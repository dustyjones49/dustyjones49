GFCI Requirements — NEC Protection Zones

How GFCIs Work

A Ground-Fault Circuit Interrupter monitors the current flowing in the hot and neutral conductors. If there's an imbalance of 4—6 milliamps (indicating current leaking to ground through a person or object), the GFCI trips within 1/30th of a second. GFCIs are available as circuit-breaker types (in the panel), receptacle types, and portable types.
