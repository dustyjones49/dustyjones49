Pipe Sizes — Residential Plumbing Reference

Understanding Pipe Sizing

Pipe sizing uses nominal dimensions that may or may not match actual inner or outer diameters depending on the material. Copper uses nominal inside diameter; PEX, PVC, and ABS use nominal trade sizes. Pipe wall thickness is specified by "schedule" — Sch 40 is the most common for standard residential use.

PEX Tubing

PEX is sized by nominal ID and available in 3/8" through 1". Most residential branch lines use 1/2" PEX; 3/4" is common for trunk lines and manifolds. PEX expands and contracts significantly with temperature — allow 1" of expansion per 100 ft for every 10°F change. Always use the manufacturer's recommended fittings.
