Toilet Rough-In Dimensions

Standard Rough-In Measurements

The toilet rough-in is the distance from the finished wall behind the toilet to the centerline of the toilet flange (the waste pipe connection in the floor). The vast majority of residential toilets use a 12-inch rough-in, which has been the industry standard for decades. This measurement is critical during the rough plumbing phase because the flange is set before drywall and flooring are installed. If the flange is placed at the wrong distance, the toilet tank may not fit against the wall or the bowl may extend too far into the room. The measurement is taken from the finished wall surface, not the framing, so it is essential to account for drywall thickness (typically 0.5 inch) plus any tile or wainscoting. A 12-inch rough-in toilet will have approximately 1 inch of clearance between the tank lid and the finished wall when installed correctly. For tight spaces or replacement applications, 10-inch rough-in toilets are available, and for larger bathrooms, 14-inch rough-in models exist but are far less common.

Clearance Requirements and Code

IRC Section P2705 requires a minimum of 15 inches of clearance measured from the centerline of the toilet to any side wall, fixture, or obstruction — totaling a minimum 30-inch wide space for a toilet compartment. The clearance in front of the toilet must be at least 21 inches (24 inches for ADA compliance). When roughing-in the flange, the center of the water supply line is typically placed 6 inches to the left of the flange centerline (when facing the toilet) and 6 to 8 inches above the finished floor. The flange itself should be mounted on top of the finished floor surface and secured to the subfloor with screws. Using a wax ring or wax-free seal, the toilet is set onto the flange and bolted through two T-bolts. Floor-mounted rear-outlet toilets use a completely different rough-in that goes through the wall rather than the floor.

  • Minimum side clearance: 15 in from centerline each side
  • Minimum front clearance: 21 in (24 in ADA)
  • Supply line offset: 6 in left, 6-8 in above floor
  • Flange must sit on top of finished floor
  • Flange inside diameter: 3 in or 4 in
  • Bolt pattern: two T-bolts at 180 degrees on flange
  • Wall-hung toilets require in-wall carrier system
