Cabinet Dimensions Reference

Base, Wall, and Tall Cabinet Sizes

Kitchen cabinetry follows decades-old dimensional standards that match appliance sizes, countertop overhangs, and human ergonomics. Base cabinets are built 34.5 inches tall so that a 1.5-inch countertop brings the finished height to 36 inches. The standard depth for base cabinets is 24 inches, measured from the back of the cabinet box to the front face (not including the door). Wall cabinets (uppers) are typically 30 or 42 inches tall and 12 to 15 inches deep, mounted with the bottom at 54 inches above the finished floor. Tall or utility cabinets reach 84 or 96 inches and are used for pantries, oven housings, or broom storage. Widths for all cabinet types range from 9 inches (spice pull-out) to 48 inches (wide sink base or pantry) in standard 3-inch increments. Over-the-range microwaves require a dedicated 30-inch wide cabinet with a 12- to 15-inch depth. Filler strips of 1 to 3 inches are used to close gaps between cabinet runs and walls, ensuring doors and drawers can open freely.

Clearance and Installation Rules

Cabinet layouts must account for door swing, appliance doors, walkway clearance, and work zones. The NKBA recommends 42 inches of walkway clearance in a one-cook kitchen and 48 inches for a multi-cook kitchen. The distance between the countertop and the bottom of the upper cabinet is 18 inches (measured from the 36-inch counter). For corners, a minimum of 12 inches of fill or a diagonal corner cabinet is needed to prevent interference between drawers and handles. Base cabinets must sit on a level platform; shims at the toe kick cover a 3.5-inch recess. Deep drawers (30 inches) have become popular for base cabinets, replacing standard drawer boxes, and require 21-inch depth cabinets to accommodate full-extension slides.

  • Upper cabinet bottom: 54 in AFF (18 in above counter)
  • Walkway minimum: 36 in (42 in recommended)
  • Corner filler: 3 in minimum (12 in for drawer clearance)
  • Toe kick recess: 3.5 in H x 3 in D
  • Over-the-range microwave: 13-18 in above cooktop
  • Sink cabinet: must clear sink bowl depth (8-10 in typical)
