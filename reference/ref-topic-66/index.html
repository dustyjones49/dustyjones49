Drawer Slide Types

Drawer Slide Selection

Drawer slides are the mechanical interface that determines how smoothly a drawer opens, how much weight it can hold, and how much of the drawer interior is accessible. Slide selection affects both the functionality of cabinetry and the cost of construction. Understanding the differences between side-mount, under-mount, center-mount, and specialty slides helps specify the right hardware for each application, from lightweight bathroom vanity drawers to heavy-duty tool chests.

Side-mount slides are the most common type in residential cabinetry. They attach to the sides of the drawer box and the inside face of the cabinet, leaving the bottom of the drawer clear. Standard side-mount slides offer three-quarter extension, meaning the drawer pulls out far enough to access most contents but about 25% of the drawer remains inside the cabinet. Full-extension side-mount slides allow the entire drawer to clear the cabinet face, providing complete access to the drawer interior. Side-mount slides are available in epoxy-coated steel for standard use and stainless steel for damp environments. Weight ratings typically range from 75 pounds for light-duty slides to 500 pounds for heavy-duty versions.

Under-mount slides mount beneath the drawer box and are invisible when the drawer is open, preserving the clean lines of the cabinet interior. They require a drawer box with specific bottom clearance and a notched back, making them more complex to install than side-mount slides. However, their concealed design and integrated soft-close mechanisms make them the premium choice for high-end kitchens and furniture. Center-mount slides consist of a single track mounted under the center of the drawer, with a guide at the back of the cabinet. They are economical and invisible when installed but have lower weight capacities and less lateral stability than paired side-mount slides. Soft-close mechanisms can be integrated into any slide type, using a hydraulic damper that catches the drawer in the last two inches of travel and pulls it closed silently.
