Real Estate Appraisal Factors

How Appraisers Determine Value

Residential real estate appraisals estimate market value using three approaches: the sales comparison approach, the cost approach, and the income approach. The sales comparison approach is the primary method for single-family homes, comparing the subject property to recently sold properties with similar characteristics. Appraisers apply adjustments for differences in key factors to derive an indicated value. Understanding which factors carry the most weight helps homeowners make renovation decisions that maximize appraisal value.

Square footage is the most objective measure of a home's size and a primary driver of value. Appraisers calculate gross living area from above-grade finished spaces, excluding garages, unfinished basements, and attics with ceiling heights below 7 feet. The value per square foot typically decreases as total square footage increases, reflecting the diminishing marginal utility of additional space. Bedroom and bathroom counts significantly influence value, with each additional bedroom typically adding 3 to 5 percent and each additional bathroom adding 5 to 7 percent in a conforming property. A home with fewer bathrooms than bedrooms will appraise lower than one with an equal or nearly equal count.

Property condition is assessed on a standardized rating scale from C1 (new construction) through C6 (uninhabitable). Most owner-occupied homes fall into C3 (well-maintained with minor updates) or C4 (adequate but showing age). The condition rating directly affects the selection of comparable sales and the magnitude of adjustments. Location is the single factor that cannot be changed and often overrides all others: a C4 home in a premier school district will appraise higher than a C2 home in a declining neighborhood. Comparable sales, or "comps," must be within the same market area, have sold within the past 6 to 12 months, and share similar physical characteristics. Appraisers typically use three to six comps, making dollar adjustments for each significant difference.
