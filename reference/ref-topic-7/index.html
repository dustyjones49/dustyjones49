Standard Vanity Sizes

Vanity Width and Depth Dimensions

Bathroom vanities are available in a wide range of standard widths to fit any bathroom size, from compact powder rooms to expansive master suites. The smallest practical vanity width is 18 inches, typically found in powder rooms or tight half-baths where space is at a premium. A single-sink vanity commonly ranges from 24 to 48 inches wide, providing landing space on both sides of the basin. Double-sink vanities start at 60 inches and go up to 72 inches or more, allowing two users to share the space comfortably. The standard cabinet depth for a bathroom vanity is 21 inches, shallower than the kitchen's 24-inch base cabinet to preserve walking space in smaller bathrooms. Countertop overhang adds another 1 to 1.5 inches, bringing the total depth to approximately 22.5 inches. Height standards are 30 to 32 inches for a seated-friendly height, though comfort-height vanities at 34.5 to 36 inches have gained popularity in recent years. When planning a bathroom layout, the vanity must not encroach on the required 30-inch clear space in front of the toilet or the 21-inch clearance in front of any fixture.

Height and Style Options

Traditional vanity heights of 30 to 32 inches match the standard seated dressing table height and work well for children and shorter adults. The newer comfort-height trend raises the cabinet to 34.5 inches (like a kitchen counter) plus countertop, reaching a total 36 inches. This height reduces back strain for taller users but may require a step stool for children. Floating vanities have become popular for contemporary designs; they require blocking in the wall during framing and often sit at 21 to 24 inches above the floor with a typical height of about 18 to 22 inches. Vessel sinks sit on top of the counter and require the vanity to be lower — typically 28 to 30 inches — so the bowl rim ends up at a comfortable 34 to 36 inches.

  • Standard height: 30 - 32 in (cabinet + countertop)
  • Comfort height: 34.5 - 36 in (cabinet + countertop)
  • Floating vanity: 21 - 24 in off the floor
  • Vessel sink vanity: 28 - 30 in tall (cabinet only)
  • Minimum front clearance: 21 in (IRC)
  • Centerline of sink from side wall: 15 in min
  • Drain rough-in: centered in sink cabinet, 16 - 20 in AFF
