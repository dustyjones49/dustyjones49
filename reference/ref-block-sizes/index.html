Concrete Block Sizes — CMU Dimensions

About Concrete Masonry Units

Concrete Masonry Units (CMU) are manufactured in standard nominal sizes that include a 3/8" mortar joint. The actual block dimensions are 3/8" less than nominal in each direction. CMUs come in various densities and strengths, with lightweight blocks weighing approximately 28—35 lbs and normal weight blocks 38—55 lbs.

Bond Beam & Lintel Blocks

Bond beam blocks have a U-shaped channel in the top to accommodate horizontal reinforcing steel and grout. They come in the same nominal sizes as standard stretchers. Lintel blocks also have a U-channel and can be pre-cast or formed on-site. Both require grout fill at every cell containing rebar.

Mortar & Grout

CMU walls use Type N or Type S mortar with a 3/8" joint. Grout is used to fill cells with rebar and must be placed in lifts not exceeding 5 feet. For 8" block, each cell requires approximately 0.5 cubic feet of grout. A typical 8×8×16 block has about 45—55% solid material by volume — the remainder is core void.
