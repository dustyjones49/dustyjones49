Light Bulb Base Types

Common Residential and Commercial Bulb Bases

The light bulb base is the connector that mechanically supports the bulb and provides the electrical connection to the fixture. The Edison screw base, named after Thomas Edison, is the most widely used type in North America. The E26 (Edison 26mm diameter) is the standard medium screw base found in nearly every table lamp, ceiling fixture, and wall sconce in the United States and Canada. In Europe and many other countries, the E27 (27mm) is the equivalent standard. The E12 candelabra base is a smaller screw base (12mm diameter) used in chandeliers, decorative sconces, night lights, and some appliance bulbs. The intermediate E17 base sits between E12 and E26 and appears in older ceiling fans and some specialty appliances. Beyond screw bases, bi-pin bases are common in halogen and LED retrofit applications. GU10 (twist-and-lock bi-pin at 10mm spacing) bulbs are widely used in track lighting and recessed downlights; they operate at line voltage (120V) and twist-lock into their socket with a quarter turn. G9 bases use two small wire loops spaced 9mm apart for compact halogen or LED capsules in under-cabinet lights and chandeliers. Pin-based linear fluorescent T8 and T5 tubes use a single-pin or bi-pin connection at each end (G13 medium bi-pin for T8 at 13mm spacing; G5 miniature bi-pin for T5 at 5mm spacing). MR16 halogen/LED reflector bulbs use GU5.3 bi-pin bases (5.3mm spacing) and operate at low voltage (12V) from a transformer.

Bulb Shape Designations

Bulb shapes use a letter-number code: the letter indicates shape type and the number indicates maximum diameter in eighths of an inch. A19 is the classic "standard bulb" shape — A for arbitrary/standard and 19 for 19/8 inches (2.375 inches diameter). BR30 and BR40 are bulged reflectors used in recessed cans (30 and 40 eighths = 3.75 and 5 inches). PAR (parabolic aluminized reflector) bulbs like PAR20, PAR30, and PAR38 are sealed-beam-style bulbs with precise beam control for outdoor floodlights and directional accent lighting. G (globe) bulbs like G25 and G40 are round decorative bulbs for bathroom vanities and pendant lights. T (tubular) bulbs range from T5 to T12 for linear tubes and from T8 to T12 for Edison-style filament bulbs. Candle-shaped C7 bulbs use an E12 base for night lights and holiday string lighting.

  • A19: standard lamp bulb, 2.375 in diameter
  • BR30: recessed can reflector, 3.75 in diameter
  • BR40: large recessed reflector, 5 in diameter
  • PAR38: outdoor flood/spot, 4.75 in diameter
  • G25: globe vanity bulb, 3.125 in diameter
  • C7: candle night light, E12 base
  • T8 linear: 1 in diameter, 4 ft length typical
