NEC 210.52 — Dwelling Unit Receptacle Outlets

The National Electrical Code (NEC) Article 210.52 specifies minimum receptacle outlet locations and spacing for habitable rooms, kitchens, bathrooms, and other areas. The primary rule is the "6-foot/12-foot rule": no point along a wall line should be more than 6 feet horizontally from a receptacle outlet, meaning receptacles are spaced no more than 12 feet apart along the wall.

AFCI & GFCI Protection

AFCI (Arc-Fault Circuit Interrupter): Required in virtually all habitable rooms per NEC 2023: living rooms, bedrooms, dining rooms, kitchens, laundry, hallways, and similar areas on 120V 15A/20A circuits. GFCI (Ground-Fault Circuit Interrupter): Required in bathrooms, kitchens, garages, crawl spaces, unfinished basements, outdoors, laundry areas, and within 6' of any sink.
