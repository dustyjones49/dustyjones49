Closet Depth Standards

Reach-In and Walk-In Closet Dimensions

Closet depth is primarily driven by the depth of a standard clothes hanger, which is approximately 17 to 18 inches from hook to shoulder tip. To allow the hanger to swing freely and the door to close without crushing garments, a reach-in closet must be at least 24 inches deep from the back wall to the inside face of the door casing. This is the industry standard and the minimum required by most residential building codes for a closet to qualify as legal storage space. Shelves above a closet rod should be set no more than 12 inches deep to avoid interfering with coat hangers. Walk-in closets need a minimum of 48 inches (4 feet) of clear depth to allow a person to walk past hanging clothes without brushing against them. If the closet is designed with hanging rods on both sides, the width should be at least 84 inches (7 feet) to provide a 36-inch aisle. Luxury walk-in closets often exceed 100 square feet and incorporate islands, seating, and dressing areas. Linen closets and utility closets require only 12 to 18 inches of depth because they hold folded items or small goods on shelves rather than hanging garments.

Closet Rod Heights and Accessories

A single clothes rod for full-length garments (dresses, coats) should be mounted at 66 to 68 inches above the finished floor. For double-rod configurations, the upper rod is placed at 80 to 84 inches for shirts and jackets while the lower rod is at 40 to 42 inches for pants and skirts. The shelf above the upper rod is typically installed at 84 inches. Wire shelving systems add about 1 inch of depth beyond the 24-inch stud cavity for reach-in closets. Built-in drawer units in walk-in closets require a 24-inch depth. Always ensure closet lighting is LED and mounted a safe distance from stored items — typically 12 inches minimum from the storage area per the NEC.

  • Single rod height: 66 - 68 in AFF (full-length items)
  • Double rod upper: 80 - 84 in AFF (shirts, jackets)
  • Double rod lower: 40 - 42 in AFF (pants, skirts)
  • Shelf above rod: 84 in AFF (12 in max depth)
  • Closet lighting: LED, min 12 in from storage (NEC 410.16)
  • Closet rod diameter: 1.25 in (wood) or 1.375 in (metal)
