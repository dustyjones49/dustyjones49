Appliance Standard Dimensions

Major Appliance Width, Depth, and Height

Residential appliances are manufactured to standard dimensions so they fit within standard cabinet openings. A standard freestanding range is 30 inches wide, 25 to 27 inches deep (excluding handles), and 36 inches tall. Slide-in ranges are 30 inches wide but have an unfinished back expecting a countertop behind them. Dishwashers are universally 24 inches wide with a 24-inch depth and 35-inch height (adjustable to 34 inches via leveling feet). Refrigerators range from 28-inch apartment models to 36-inch standard French-door units with a depth of 30 to 36 inches (counter-depth models are shallower at 26 to 28 inches without handles). Front-load washers and dryers are 27 inches wide, 30 to 33 inches deep, and 39 inches tall. Top-load washers are usually narrower (27 inches) but taller (42-44 inches) to accommodate agitator mechanisms. Stackable washer-dryer combinations require a stacked clearance of about 78 inches. Appliance garage cabinets must account for ventilation clearances specified in each manufacturer's installation manual.

Clearance and Utility Requirements

Every appliance requires specific clearances for ventilation, door swing, and service access. Refrigerators need 1 inch of space above and 0.5 to 1 inch on each side for heat dissipation; integrated or built-in models may require bottom vent grilles. Ranges need 30 inches of clearance above the cooking surface to combustible materials unless a range hood provides protection. Gas ranges require a gas shutoff valve within 6 feet and a dedicated 120V outlet. Electric ranges need a 240V 50A circuit. Dishwashers need a 20A circuit, hot water supply line, and drain connection to the sink drain or garbage disposal. Washers require hot and cold water supply and a 2-inch standpipe for the drain. Dryers need a 4-inch rigid metal exhaust duct no more than 35 feet in developed length and a 240V 30A circuit for electric models or a gas line for gas models.

  • Refrigerator: 1 in top clearance, 0.5-1 in side clearance
  • Range: 30 in clearance to combustibles above cooktop
  • Dishwasher: 20A circuit, hot water line, drain line
  • Electric Dryer: 240V 30A circuit, 4-in rigid metal vent
  • Gas Dryer: 120V 15A circuit, gas line, 4-in rigid metal vent
  • Range Hood: 18-30 in above electric, 24-30 in above gas
