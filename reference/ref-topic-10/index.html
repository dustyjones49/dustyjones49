Hallway Width Code

Hallway Width Requirements

Hallway width is governed by building codes that ensure safe egress and accessibility. The International Residential Code (IRC) specifies a minimum hallway width of 36 inches for all habitable floor levels. This dimension is measured between the finished wall surfaces, not the rough framing. While 36 inches is the legal minimum, it creates a tight passage where two people cannot comfortably pass each other and furniture delivery becomes challenging. A 42-inch hallway provides a more comfortable experience and is widely considered the practical minimum for new construction. For homes designed with universal design or ADA-compliant intent, 48 inches is required to permit a 180-degree wheelchair turn and a 60-inch-diameter turning circle at dead ends. Commercial buildings governed by the International Building Code (IBC) require hallways to be a minimum of 44 inches wide in most occupancies, with wider dimensions for assembly and healthcare uses. Stairway landings must be at least as deep as the stair is wide, and the minimum stair width for residential is 36 inches (above the handrail projection).

Egress and Design Considerations

Hallways that serve as egress paths must maintain their required width without obstruction. Furniture, wall sconces, and protruding trim cannot reduce the clear width below the minimum. Wall-mounted handrails are permitted provided they do not project more than 4.5 inches into the required width. For hallways longer than 50 feet, the NEC requires at least one receptacle outlet. Lighting must be provided at each section with switches at each end. Dead-end corridors in residential construction are limited to 20 feet in length per the IRC. In multi-story homes, the stairway and connecting hallway should form a continuous egress path at least 36 inches wide from the uppermost habitable floor to the exit door.

  • Clear width measured between finished wall surfaces
  • Handrail projection: max 4.5 in into the required width
  • Receptacles: at least one every 50 ft (NEC 210.52)
  • Lighting: switch at each end of hall (IRC E3903.3)
  • Dead-end max: 20 ft (IRC R311.7)
  • Headroom: 80 in minimum above finished floor (IRC R305.1)
