Door Hardware Finishes

Choosing Hardware Finishes

Door hardware finishes are the jewelry of a home, defining the look of every door, cabinet, and plumbing fixture. The finish must coordinate across all visible hardware elements in a room for a cohesive design. Finish durability varies significantly: some living finishes develop a natural patina over time while others are engineered to resist wear, tarnish, and corrosion. The right choice balances aesthetic preference with the practical demands of the installation location.

Satin nickel is the most popular finish in contemporary residential construction. It has a warm silver tone with a brushed texture that hides fingerprints and water spots better than polished finishes. Satin nickel is lacquer-coated to prevent tarnishing and pairs well with both warm and cool color palettes. Oil rubbed bronze is a dark, almost black finish with copper undertones that show through in highlights. It is a living finish, meaning it wears naturally over time in high-contact areas, developing a unique patina that many homeowners find desirable. It is strongly associated with traditional, Tuscan, and Old World design styles. Polished brass delivers a bright, reflective gold tone that was ubiquitous in the 1980s and early 1990s.

Matte black is a bold contemporary finish that has surged in popularity over the past decade. It provides high contrast against light-colored doors and walls, making hardware a deliberate design statement. Quality matte black finishes are applied through physical vapor deposition or powder coating for durability; lower-quality painted finishes may chip and reveal the base metal underneath. Polished chrome offers a bright, mirror-like silver finish that is highly durable and easy to clean. It is the standard for modern and Art Deco interiors and is virtually required in bathrooms with chrome plumbing fixtures. Chrome's reflective surface shows every fingerprint and water spot, requiring more frequent cleaning than brushed finishes to maintain its mirror-like appearance.
