Concrete PSI — Compressive Strength Ratings

Understanding Concrete Strength

Concrete compressive strength is measured in pounds per square inch (PSI) at 28 days of curing. The mix design — ratio of cement, aggregate, water, and admixtures — determines the final strength. Higher PSI concrete has lower water-to-cement ratios and typically more cementitious material per cubic yard. Air-entrainment (4—7%) is standard for exterior concrete in freeze-thaw climates.
