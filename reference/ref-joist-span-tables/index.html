How to Use Span Tables

Span tables specify the maximum allowable distance a joist or rafter can span between supports based on species, grade, size, spacing, and design loads. These reference values assume 40 psf live load + 10 psf dead load for residential floors (sleeping rooms may use 30 psf live). Always check local amendments; some jurisdictions have stricter requirements.

Engineered Lumber Spans (LVL, I-Joists)

Engineered lumber products (LVL beams, I-joists, LSL, PSL) can span significantly farther than solid sawn lumber. Typical I-joists can span 16—26 feet at 16" OC spacing, depending on depth (9-1/2" to 16") and flange width. Always refer to the manufacturer's specific span tables — values vary widely by product line. LVL beams for headers typically follow manufacturer charts based on width, depth, and load conditions.
