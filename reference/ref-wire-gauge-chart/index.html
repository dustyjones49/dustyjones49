AWG Wire Gauge Chart — Ampacity & Breaker Sizing

About Wire Gauges

American Wire Gauge (AWG) is the standard for electrical wire sizing in North America. Lower AWG numbers indicate thicker wire capable of carrying more current. NEC Table 310.16 governs ampacity ratings based on copper conductor type and temperature rating. Always use 60°C column for circuits under 100A unless all terminations are rated for 75°C.

Voltage Drop Considerations

For runs over 100 feet, upsize wire by one gauge to compensate for voltage drop (keep drop under 3% for branch circuits). Voltage drop = 2 × K × I × L / CMA, where K = 12.9 for copper, I = amps, L = one-way length in feet, and CMA = circular mil area of the conductor.
