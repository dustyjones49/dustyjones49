Drywall Sizes — Sheet Dimensions & Thickness Guide

Standard Drywall Dimensions

Drywall (gypsum board) comes in standard sheet sizes and thicknesses optimized for different applications. The most common sheet size is 4' × 8', but longer lengths up to 16' are available for commercial use. Thickness ranges from 1/4" to 5/8".

Edge Profiles

Tapered edge: Thin recess along long edges to accommodate joint tape and compound — standard for finishing. Square edge: Flat edges, used for texture finishes or laminating. Beveled edge: Found on some specialty panels. Tongue & groove: Interlocking edges for subfloor/underlayment panels.
