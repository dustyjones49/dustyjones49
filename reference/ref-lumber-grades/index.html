Lumber Grades — Structural & Appearance Classifications

Understanding Lumber Grading

Softwood lumber is graded according to the American Softwood Lumber Standard (PS 20) by certified grading agencies. Grades are based on visual inspection or machine stress rating (MSR). Visual grades consider knot size and frequency, slope of grain, shake, wane, and other natural characteristics. MSR uses non-destructive mechanical testing.
