Plywood Grades — APA Veneer Classifications

The APA Grading System

Plywood is graded by the APA (Engineered Wood Association) using two letter grades: one for the face veneer and one for the back. For example, "A-C" means A-grade face, C-grade back. The grading considers natural defects (knots, splits), manufacturing repairs (plugs, patches), and veneer smoothness. Higher grades are better for visible applications.
