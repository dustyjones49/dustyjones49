Tile Sizes — Floor & Wall Tile Dimensions

Tile Sizing Standards

Tile sizes have evolved dramatically, from traditional mosaics to large-format panels. Modern tile is measured in both imperial and metric. Nominal sizes often include a slight allowance for grout joints, though actual manufacturing dimensions are typically precise. Tile thickness varies by material and format.

Grout Joint Sizing

Grout joint width varies with tile size and type. Finished/rectified tiles allow 1/16" to 1/8" joints. Standard ceramic/porcelain typically uses 1/8" to 1/4" joints. Irregular (tumbled, handmade) tiles require 3/16" to 3/8" joints. Larger tiles and butt-joint installations generally require tighter tolerances and perfectly flat substrates.
