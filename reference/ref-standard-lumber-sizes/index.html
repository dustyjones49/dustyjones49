Standard Lumber Sizes — Nominal vs. Actual Dimensions

Understanding Lumber Sizing

Lumber is sold by "nominal" dimensions — the rough-sawn size before drying and planing. The "actual" finished dimensions are smaller. This reference covers all standard dimensional lumber used in framing and finish carpentry, following industry standards set by the American Lumber Standard Committee.

Length Standards

Dimensional lumber is typically sold in even-foot lengths from 8 ft to 24 ft. Common increments are 8', 10', 12', 14', 16', 18', 20'. Stud-length 2×4s are commonly 92-5/8" (pre-cut for 8' walls with a double top plate and sole plate). For lengths over 20 ft, availability varies by region and lumberyard.
