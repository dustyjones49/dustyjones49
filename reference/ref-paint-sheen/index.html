Paint Sheen Guide — Flat to High Gloss

What Is Paint Sheen?

Paint sheen (or gloss level) refers to how much light reflects off the painted surface. Sheen is measured in gloss units at a 60-degree angle (ASTM D523 standard). Lower-sheen paints hide surface imperfections better; higher-sheen paints are more durable and washable. The right sheen depends on the room's function, surface condition, and traffic level.
