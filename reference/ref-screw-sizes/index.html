Screw Sizes — Gauges, Lengths & Pilot Hole Guide

Understanding Screw Sizing

Wood screws are sized by gauge number (#) which indicates shank diameter, followed by length in inches. Higher gauge numbers mean thicker screws. For example, a #8 × 1-1/2" screw has a #8 shank diameter and is 1-1/2 inches long. Drywall and specialty screws follow different conventions.

Head Types & Drive Styles

Flat head: Countersunk, sits flush — standard for woodworking. Pan head: Rounded top, used with washers for metal-to-metal connections. Trim head: Small countersunk head for finish applications. Drive styles: Phillips (#2 most common), Torx/Star (T15, T20, T25), Square/Robertson (#1, #2), and Hex. Torx and square drives reduce cam-out compared to Phillips.
