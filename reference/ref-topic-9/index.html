Garage Size Standards

Residential Garage Dimensions

Standard garage sizes are dictated by the dimensions of typical vehicles and the minimum comfortable clearances around them. A single-car garage is traditionally 12 feet wide by 20 to 22 feet deep, which accommodates a sedan or small SUV with just enough room to walk around the vehicle. For modern larger vehicles like full-size pickup trucks and SUVs, a 14-foot width and 24-foot depth is strongly recommended. A two-car garage should be at least 20 feet wide by 20 feet deep but benefits greatly from a 24-foot width and 24-foot depth, which allows two average vehicles to park side-by-side with doors fully opening without striking each other or the walls. Three-car garages typically measure 30 to 36 feet wide by 22 to 24 feet deep. The garage door opening for a single bay is 8 or 9 feet wide and 7 or 8 feet tall. Double-width doors for two-car bays are 16 feet wide. Ceiling height in a garage is typically 8 to 10 feet, though 12-foot ceilings are common when vehicle lifts are planned. The garage floor must slope toward the overhead door at 0.25 inch per foot for drainage per IRC R309.

Garage Door Opening Sizes

Standard residential garage doors are sold in 8-foot and 9-foot widths for single bays and 16-foot widths for double bays. Height options are 7 feet (standard) and 8 feet (for taller vehicles). The rough opening for a garage door is the same width as the door but adds 1 to 2 inches for leveling. The header above the opening must be sized per structural span tables — typically a double 2x12 for an 8-foot opening up to a 4x14 LVL or glulam for a 16-foot opening. Torsion springs require 12 inches of headroom above the opening for standard lift, and up to 24 inches for high-lift tracks that follow a sloped ceiling.

  • Single door opening: 8 x 7 ft or 9 x 7 ft (standard)
  • Double door opening: 16 x 7 ft or 16 x 8 ft
  • Headroom: 12 in (standard lift), 15-24 in (high lift)
  • Sideroom: 3.5 in minimum each side
  • Backroom: door height + 18 in minimum for opener
  • Floor slope: 0.25 in per ft toward door (IRC R309.1)
