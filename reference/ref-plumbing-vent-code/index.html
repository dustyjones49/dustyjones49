Plumbing Vent Code — IPC 2021 Vent Requirements

Purpose of Venting

Plumbing vents serve three essential functions: they admit air to balance pressure in the drain-waste system (preventing trap siphonage), they allow sewer gases to escape above the roof, and they provide an oxygen supply for aerobic bacteria in septic systems. Every fixture trap must be protected by a vent within critical distance.
