Formula

Step 1: Board Feet per Piece = (T(in) × W(in) × L(ft)) / 12
Step 2: Total Board Feet = BF per Piece × Quantity
Step 3: Total Cost = Total BF × Price per BF

BF = 2×8×10/12 = 13.33 BF. Ten boards = 133 BF.

Frequently Asked Questions

A board foot is 144 cubic inches of wood - that's a piece 1 inch thick, 12 inches wide and 12 inches long. For hardwood lumber, the formula is (thickness in inches × width in inches × length in feet) Ã· 12. Rough lumber is sold in quarter-inch increments: 4/4 = 1 in, 8/4 = 2 in.

4/4 is 1 inch thick, 5/4 is 1.25 inches, 6/4 is 1.5 inches, 8/4 is 2 inches. These are rough sawn dimensions - surfacing removes about 1/8 to 1/4 inch per face. So a 4/4 board dresses to about 3/4 inch actual.

A 20-inch diameter hardwood tree with 40 ft of merchantable trunk yields roughly 250-400 board feet. But recovery rate is 50-60% after sawing, drying and planing. Always buy 20% extra rough lumber to account for defects and waste.
