Formula

Example Calculation

cf = 8 × 4 × (12 / 12) = 32 cu ft
With 15% compaction = 32 × 1.15 = 36.8 cu ft
cy = 36.8 / 27 = 1.36 cubic yards → order 1.5 yards.

Frequently Asked Questions

How deep should topsoil be for a garden?

Vegetables and new lawns need 4–6 inches of quality topsoil over subsoil for root growth. Raised beds do best with 10–12 inches. For topdressing an existing lawn, 1 inch is enough — more will smother grass.

How many yards of topsoil per 100 sq ft?

At 3 inches deep you need 0.93 cubic yards per 100 sq ft, at 6 inches deep you need 1.85 yards. That equals about 1.4 tons moist per yard. Our calculator converts instantly from inches to yards.

Does topsoil compact after delivery?

Yes, topsoil settles 15–20% after watering and rain as air pockets collapse. Order 10% extra and let it settle a week before planting. Lightly till the top 2 inches to break up crust before seeding.
