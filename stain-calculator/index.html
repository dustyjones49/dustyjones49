Formula

area = L × W × sides
total_sqft = area × coats
gallons = total_sqft / coverage
gallons_to_buy = ceil(gallons)

Example Calculation

A 100 ft fence, 6 ft tall, both sides, 1 coat, 250 sq ft/gal:
Area per coat = 100 × 6 × 2 = 1,200 sq ft
Total to cover = 1200 × 1 = 1,200 sq ft
Gallons (exact) = 1200 / 250 = 4.80 gal
Gallons to buy = ceil(4.80) = 5 gallons

FAQ

Smooth cedar or pine covers 250-350 sq ft per gallon, rough sawn cedar only 150-200 sq ft per gallon because it soaks up more. For a 6 ft privacy fence count both sides - 100 linear ft is 1200 sq ft.
Most transparent stains need 1 coat, semi-transparent 1-2 coats, and solid color 2 coats for even color and UV protection. Don't over-apply - thick coats peel. Apply thin and work into wood.
Most exterior stains are stain and sealer in one and don't need extra topcoat. If using interior stain outside, add spar urethane sealer. For decks, use a penetrating oil sealer with UV blocker and re-coat every 2-3 years.
