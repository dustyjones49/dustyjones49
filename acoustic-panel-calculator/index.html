FAQ

Cover 25-50% of total surface area for general listening rooms. Home theaters benefit from 40-60%. Studios may need 50%+. Start with 25% and add as needed.

Focus on first reflection points on side walls, the wall behind speakers, corners for bass traps, and the ceiling cloud above the listening position.

Standard panels are 2x4 ft (8 sq ft each). They come in thicknesses from 1-4 inches. Thicker panels absorb lower frequencies better.
