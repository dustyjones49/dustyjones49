FAQ

27 cubic feet = 1 cubic yard. Picture a 3×3×3 ft cube. Divide your cubic feet by 27. For example, 81 cu ft = 3 cu yd. Most bulk materials like concrete, gravel, and mulch are sold by the cubic yard.

One cubic yard covers 324 sq ft at 1 inch deep, 162 sq ft at 2 in, 108 sq ft at 3 in, and 81 sq ft at 4 in. Use this for quick estimates when ordering mulch or gravel for a known area.
