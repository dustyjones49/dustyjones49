FAQ

A linear foot measures length only—12 inches regardless of width. Trim, baseboard, and molding are sold by the linear foot. A 10-foot board is 10 linear feet whether it's 2 or 12 inches wide. It's the simplest form of measurement.

Linear feet = length only (1 dimension). Square feet = area (L×W, 2 dimensions). Flooring is sold by sq ft; trim by linear ft. To estimate sq ft from linear ft: divide by the width in feet. Example: 100 linear ft of 6-inch boards = 50 sq ft.
