Stock: $200-300/lf (Home Depot, IKEA). Semi-custom: $300-600/lf. Custom: $600-1,200+/lf. A standard kitchen with 24 lf of cabinets: $4,800 to $29,000. Installation adds $100-200/lf. Our calculator gives you quick estimates for budgeting.

Base cabinets sit on the floor and hold the countertop—typically 24 in deep and 34.5 in tall. Wall cabinets mount above the counter at 18 in clearance, are 12-15 in deep, and come in 30, 36 or 42 in heights. A standard kitchen has about 60% base and 40% wall.
