Formula

area = L × W × pitchFactor
squares = ceil(area / 100)
bundles = squares × 3
ridgeCap = L (linear feet)

Example Calculation

A 24 × 40 ft house with 6/12 pitch:
Area = 24 × 40 × 1.118 = 1,073.28 sq ft
Squares = ceil(1073.28 / 100) = 11 squares
Bundles = 11 × 3 = 33 bundles
Ridge cap = 24 linear ft

FAQ

One roofing square is 100 sq ft of finished roof. Architectural shingles are 3 bundles per square, while some 3-tab shingles are 3-4 bundles per square depending on brand. Check the wrapper for coverage.
Multiply footprint area by pitch factor: 4/12 = 1.054, 6/12 = 1.118, 8/12 = 1.202, 12/12 = 1.414. Steeper pitch means more shingle area than footprint. Our calculator applies this automatically.
Add 10% waste for simple gable roofs and 15% for hips, valleys and dormers. Complex roofs with lots of cuts can need 20% extra. Rounding up to the next full square already adds some waste.
