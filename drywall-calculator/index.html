Frequently Asked Questions

How many drywall sheets for a 12×12 room?

A 12×12×8 room needs about 12 sheets of 4×8 drywall for walls (448 sq ft) plus 5 sheets for ceiling (144 sq ft) = 17 sheets before waste. With 10% waste order 19 sheets. Use 4×12 sheets if you have help to reduce seams.

What size drywall sheet should I use?

4×8 sheets are easy for DIY and fit through doors. 4×12 sheets mean fewer butt joints on tall walls and faster finishing but require two people and a lift for ceilings. Half-inch is standard for walls and ceilings.

How many screws per drywall sheet?

Use about 32 screws per 4×8 sheet — screws every 12 in on ceilings and 16 in on walls. That's roughly 28 screws per field and edge pattern. One pound box of 1-1/4 in coarse thread covers about 10 sheets.
