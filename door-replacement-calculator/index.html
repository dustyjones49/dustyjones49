FAQ

Standard exterior door replacement runs $1,500-$3,000 installed. Fiberglass doors cost more but offer better insulation. Steel doors are durable and mid-range.

Interior pre-hung doors are DIY-friendly. Exterior doors require proper flashing, leveling, and weather sealing. Mistakes lead to air leaks and water damage.

A professional can replace an interior door in 2-3 hours. Exterior doors take 4-6 hours. Sliding patio doors may take a full day due to framing adjustments.
