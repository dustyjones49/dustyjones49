Frequently Asked Questions

Most crushed stone weighs 1.4 tons per cubic yard, pea gravel about 1.3 tons, and crusher run up to 1.5 tons. We use 1.4 as the default because it's the most common for driveways. Multiply cubic yards by density to get tons.

A new driveway needs 6-8 inches total: 4-6 inches of large 2-3 in crusher run base compacted, then 2-3 inches of 3/4 in crushed stone on top. For a top-up on existing base, 3-4 inches is enough. Always compact in 2-3 in lifts.

Use 3/4 in crushed angular stone with fines (often called 3/4 in crusher run or road base) for the base - it locks together and doesn't shift. Top with 1/2-3/4 in clean crushed for drainage and grip. Avoid round pea gravel for driveways as it moves under tires.
