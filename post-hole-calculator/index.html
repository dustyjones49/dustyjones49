FAQ

How deep should a post hole be for a 6 ft fence?
Dig 30-36 inches deep for a 6 ft fence in most climates, or below local frost line in cold areas (42+ inches). Add 6 inches of gravel at bottom for drainage. Depth should be at least one-third of above-ground post height plus 6 inches.
What diameter post hole for 4x4 post?
Use a 10-12 inch diameter hole for a 4x4 post - roughly 3 times post width for good concrete collar. For 6x6 posts use 12-14 inch diameter. This gives enough bearing and prevents frost heave.
How many bags of concrete per post hole?
A 10 in diameter by 36 in deep hole needs 1.64 cu ft of concrete, which is 5 bags of 50lb fast-set or 3 bags of 80lb. Our calculator multiplies by hole count so you can order exact pallets.
