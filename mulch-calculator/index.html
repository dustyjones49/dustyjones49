Formula

Step 1: Cubic Feet = L × W × (D/12) × (1 + Waste/100)
Step 2: Cubic Yards = Cubic Feet / 27
Step 3: 2 cu ft Bags = ceil(Cubic Feet / 2)

Frequently Asked Questions

Maintain 2-3 inches for established beds and 3-4 inches for new beds to block weeds and retain moisture. Never pile mulch against tree trunks or house siding - leave a 3-4 inch gap to prevent rot and pests. Fluff it each spring.

One cubic yard covers 324 sq ft at 1 inch deep, 162 sq ft at 2 inches, and 108 sq ft at 3 inches deep. That's about 13.5 bags of 2 cu ft mulch per yard. Use the calculator to avoid short loads.

Shredded hardwood mulch decomposes in 12 months and should be topped up annually in spring. Dyed mulch fades faster. Rake and turn existing mulch before adding a thin 1 inch fresh layer to keep it from matting.
