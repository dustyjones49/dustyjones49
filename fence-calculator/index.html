Formula

Step 1: Posts = ceil(L / Spacing) + 1 + Gates
Step 2: Rails = Posts × 3
Step 3: Pickets = ceil((L × 12) / (Picket Width + Gap))

Example Calculation

posts = 14, pickets = 218. Add a gate post if needed.

Frequently Asked Questions

With standard 5.5 in dog-eared pickets and no gap, you need about 2.18 pickets per linear foot. For a 100 ft fence that's 218 pickets. With a 0.5 in shadowbox gap, you need about 2 pickets per foot.

Set posts one-third of total length in ground plus 6 inches of gravel for drainage. For a 6 ft fence use 8 ft posts set 30-36 inches deep, or deeper than frost line in cold climates (36-42 in). Use concrete collar top 8 inches below grade.

Most residential privacy fences use 8 ft on-center spacing for economy and strength. Use 6 ft spacing for high wind areas or heavy gates. Always double posts at corners and gates for extra support.
