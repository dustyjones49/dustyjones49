Formula

area_sqin = L × W × 144
tile_area = (tL + gap) × (tW + gap)
tiles_clean = ceil(area_sqin / tile_area)
tiles_with_waste = ceil(tiles_clean × (1 + waste% / 100))

Example Calculation

A 12 × 12 ft room with 12×12 in tile, 0.125 in gap, 10% waste:
Room area = 12 × 12 × 144 = 20,736 in²
Tile area = (12 + 0.125) × (12 + 0.125) = 147.02 in²
Tiles (clean) = ceil(20736 / 147.02) = 141 tiles
Tiles (10% waste) = ceil(141 × 1.10) = 155 tiles

FAQ

Buy 10% extra for straight lay and 15% for diagonal or large format tile over 12 inches. This covers cuts, breakage and future repairs. Keep 2-3 extra tiles stored for future replacements.
Use 1/8 inch gap for most rectified tile and 3/16 inch for non-rectified or natural stone. Small mosaic tiles under 2 inches use 1/16 inch. Larger grout joints are more forgiving but show dirt more.
Most 12x12 tile boxes contain 10-12 pieces covering 10-12 sq ft. 12x24 tiles cover 10-15 sq ft per box. Check the box label for exact coverage. Always buy by the box and return unopened extras.
