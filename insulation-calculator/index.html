FAQ

What R-value do I need for walls and attic?
Use R-13 batts for 2x4 walls, R-19 or R-21 for 2x6 walls, and R-38 to R-49 for attics depending on climate zone. Colder zones need higher R-values. Check DOE map for your zip code - attics are the best bang for buck.
How many insulation batts per 1000 sq ft?
At 15 sq ft per batt pack, you need 67 packs per 1000 sq ft. For 2x4 walls that's R-13, for attics R-38 batts cover less area per bag (about 30 sq ft). Always buy faced for walls and unfaced for second attic layer.
Do you need vapor barrier with insulation?
In cold climates use faced batts with kraft paper facing the warm interior side to act as vapor retarder. In hot-humid climates use unfaced plus separate poly if required by code. Never double vapor barrier or you trap moisture.
