FAQ

A 12 inch diameter column 4 ft tall needs about 3.1 cu ft of concrete—that's 6 bags of 80lb. For taller columns, multiply by height. Our calculator handles any height and multiple columns at once.
Minimum 4 vertical bars for columns up to 12 inches wide, 6 bars for 14-18 inch columns, 8 bars for 20+ inches. Ties at 12 in spacing for most columns, 6 in at top and bottom 2 ft for seismic zones.
Use 4000 PSI for most residential columns (decks, porches). 3500 PSI minimum for any structural column. 5000 PSI for commercial or heavy load columns. Add fiber reinforcement for crack resistance.
