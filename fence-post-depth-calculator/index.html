FAQ

One-third of the above-ground height plus 6 inches for gravel. Common rules: 6 ft fence = 30-36 in deep, 4 ft fence = 24 in deep, 8 ft fence = 36-42 in deep. Always go below frost line. Deeper posts resist wind better.
A 10 in × 36 in hole needs 1.64 cu ft = 5 bags of 50lb. An 80lb bag fills 0.6 cu ft so 3 bags. Always buy one extra bag per job. Mix concrete fairly dry—too wet and posts can lean before it sets.
For privacy fences and gates: concrete is best—it locks posts and resists wind. For rural or farm fencing: compacted gravel is enough and allows posts to be replaced easily. Use fast-set concrete that sets in 20-40 minutes.
