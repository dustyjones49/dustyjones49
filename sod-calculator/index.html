Formula

area = L × W − subtractArea
pallets = ceil(area / 500)
total_sqft = pallets × 500

Example Calculation

A 60 × 40 ft lawn minus 200 sq ft of beds:
Area = (60 × 40) − 200 = 2,200 sq ft
Pallets = ceil(2200 / 500) = 5 pallets
Total to buy = 5 × 500 = 2,500 sq ft

FAQ

Most sod pallets cover 400-600 sq ft depending on farm and region. We use 500 sq ft as a safe average, which is about 50 rolls at 2x5 ft each. Always confirm with your local supplier before ordering.
Water new sod daily with 1 inch of water for the first 2 weeks to keep roots moist but not soggy. Then water every other day week 3, and twice a week after that. Avoid walking on it for 2-3 weeks.
