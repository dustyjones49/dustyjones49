FAQ

Vinyl: $6,000 installed. Wood: $9,000 installed. Fiberglass: $12,000 installed. Bulk discounts often apply for 5+ windows. Energy-efficient options carry tax credits.

Vinyl is affordable and low maintenance. Wood offers classic aesthetics but needs painting. Fiberglass is strongest and most energy efficient. All three offer good R-values.

A crew can replace 5-10 windows in a day. Each window takes 30-60 minutes. Full-house replacement (15+ windows) typically takes 2-3 days.
