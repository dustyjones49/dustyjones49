FAQ

Granite: $40-100/sq ft installed. Quartz: $50-120/sq ft. Marble: $60-150. Laminate: $20-40. Butcher block: $30-60. A typical kitchen with 30 sq ft of counters costs $1,200-3,600. Edge profiles and cutouts add cost.

Standard depth is 25.5 inches including the 1.5 inch overhang past 24-inch base cabinets. Island tops are deeper at 36-42 inches. Peninsulas may extend 48+ inches. Backsplash adds 4-18 inches of height and is priced separately by sq ft.
