FAQ

45 mil EPDM rubber liner is standard—lasts 20-30 years. PVC liners are 20 mil, cheaper but degrade in 5-10 years from UV. Always use underlayment fabric beneath the liner. 1-2 ft overlap on all edges hidden with coping stones or plants.
