FAQ

How many tons of asphalt per square foot?
At 3 inches thick you need about 0.037 tons per sq ft, or 1 ton per 27 sq ft. At 2 inches thick it's 1 ton per 40 sq ft. Our calculator uses compacted density of 2 tons per cubic yard for accurate estimates.
How thick should an asphalt driveway be?
Residential driveways should be 2-3 inches of hot mix over 6-8 inches of compacted crushed base. Heavy use or clay soils need 3-4 inches of asphalt. Commercial parking lots are 3-4 inches minimum.
How long before you can seal new asphalt?
Wait 6-12 months before first sealcoat so oils can cure and surface can harden. Sealing too early traps oils and causes peeling. After that, reseal every 3-5 years to protect from UV and oil stains.
