Formula

Step 1: Cubic Feet = L × W × (D/12) × (1 + Waste/100)
Step 2: Cubic Yards = Cubic Feet / 27
Step 3: 80lb Bags = ceil(Cubic Feet / 0.6)
Step 4: Total Cost = Cubic Yards × Cost per Yard

Frequently Asked Questions

One cubic yard is 27 cubic feet and an 80lb bag yields about 0.6 cu ft, so you need 45 bags per yard. For a 60lb bag it's 60 bags per yard. Always round up and add 5-10% waste for spillage.

For patios, walkways and shed bases 4 inches is standard and handles foot traffic and light furniture. Driveways, garage floors and RV pads should be 6 inches thick with #4 rebar at 18 in on center for heavy loads and freeze-thaw cycles.

Concrete reaches about 60% strength in 7 days and full design strength in 28 days. You can walk on it after 24-48 hours and drive after 7 days for a 4 in slab. Keep it moist and covered for the first 3 days for best curing.
