Frequently Asked Questions

Buy 5-10% extra for straight lay, 15% for diagonal or herringbone patterns. For rooms with lots of cuts like hallways or closets, go 15%. One extra box is smart for future repairs — styles get discontinued.

Laminate and vinyl plank boxes typically contain 15-25 sq ft. Hardwood boxes range from 18-24 sq ft. Engineered wood is usually 20-30 sq ft per carton. Always check the box label before calculating — the default is 20 sq ft.

Laminate is cheapest at $1-3/sq ft, good for rentals and low-traffic areas. Vinyl plank is 100% waterproof ($2-5/sq ft), best for kitchens, bathrooms and basements. Hardwood ($4-12/sq ft) adds the most resale value but can't handle moisture. Engineered wood splits the difference with a real wood veneer.
