Common Questions

  • How do calculations work? All calculators run entirely on your device. Enter your values and the result appears instantly. No internet connection is required.
  • Are the results accurate? Results are estimates for reference and planning. Always verify measurements and consult your local building codes or a qualified professional for final decisions.
  • How do I save a calculator? Tap the star (★) on a calculator to add it to your favorites for quick access.
  • Why did my inputs disappear? Your values are saved locally on your device. Clearing your app or browser data will remove them.
  • Does the app collect my data? No. FieldMath does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. See our Privacy Policy.
