Frequently Asked Questions

How many gallons in a 16x32 pool?
A 16x32 ft rectangular pool with 3-6 ft depth (avg 4.5 ft) holds about 17,200 gallons. With 4 ft flat bottom = 15,300 gallons. Pool chemicals are dosed per 10,000 gallons so accurate volume matters for safety and cost.
How to calculate average pool depth?
Average depth = (shallow depth + deep depth) / 2. For a 3 ft shallow and 8 ft deep end, average = 5.5 ft. More accurate: measure depth at 3 points along the length and average those.
How much does it cost to fill a pool?
At municipal water rates of $0.005-0.01 per gallon, filling a 15,000 gallon pool costs $75-150. Have water delivered by truck ($500-1500) if you're on a well to avoid burning out the pump.
