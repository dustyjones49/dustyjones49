Frequently Asked Questions

How much grout per square foot?
About 0.1-0.15 lbs per square foot for standard 12x12 tile with 1/8 in joints. For mosaic or smaller tiles, grout use can double. Our calculator estimates based on your exact tile and joint sizes.
Sanded vs unsanded grout?
Use sanded grout for joints 1/8 in and wider — it's stronger and shrinks less. Use unsanded grout only for joints under 1/8 in or polished stone that might scratch. Epoxy grout is best for wet areas and costs 3x more.
How long does grout take to dry?
Grout sets in 24 hours and fully cures in 7 days. Don't walk on floors for 24 hours. Seal after 72 hours for sanded grout. Lightly mist with water twice daily for first 3 days for maximum strength.
