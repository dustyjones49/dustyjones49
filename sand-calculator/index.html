FAQ

A standard 8×8 ft sandbox at 6 inches deep needs about 1.19 cubic yards or 1.5 tons of play sand. That's about 60 bags of 50lb play sand. Add a cover to keep cats out and sand dry between play sessions.
Use sharp concrete sand (not play sand) for the 1-inch bedding layer under pavers. It's angular and locks together under compaction. Play sand is too round and soft—pavers will shift. Add polymeric sand in the joints after laying.
Gravel (3/4 in crushed) goes as the 4-6 in base layer. Sand goes as the 1 in bedding layer on top to level pavers. Never use sand as the main base—it washes out. The gravel base is load-bearing, sand is for leveling.
