Formula

barsWide = ceil((W × 12 ÷ sp) + 1)
barsLong = ceil((L × 12 ÷ sp) + 1)
total ft = (barsWide × L) + (barsLong × W)

Example Calculation

Slab 20 ft × 20 ft at 18 in grid, #4 rebar

Bars wide = ceil(20 × 12 ÷ 18) + 1 = 14 bars
Bars long = ceil(20 × 12 ÷ 18) + 1 = 14 bars
Total feet = (14 × 20) + (14 × 20) = 560 ft
Sticks = 560 ÷ 20 = 28 sticks
Weight = 560 × 0.668 = 374.08 lbs

FAQ

What rebar spacing for concrete slab?
For driveways and patios use #4 rebar at 18 inches on-center each way, placed mid-slab on chairs. For structural slabs or thickened edges use 12 inches on-center. Light sidewalks can use welded wire mesh at 6x6 W1.4 instead.
What is lap length for rebar?
Minimum lap splice is 12 inches or 30 times bar diameter, whichever is greater. For #4 rebar that's 15 inches. In walls and footings use 24 inches lap. Add 10% extra material for laps in your total.
What size rebar for driveway?
Use #4 (1/2 inch diameter) rebar for most residential driveways and slabs 4-6 inches thick. #3 is too light for vehicle loads, #5 is overkill unless engineered. Space it 18 in oc and keep 2 inches clear from soil and top.
