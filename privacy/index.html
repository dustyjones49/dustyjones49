Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

FieldMath ("we", "us", or "our") provides this app as a free tool for construction and DIY calculations. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect and how it is used.

Information We Collect

FieldMath is designed to work entirely on your device. We do not require you to create an account, and we do not collect personal information such as your name, email address, or location to use the app.

FieldMath itself does not collect or transmit usage data. However, because the apps show ads served by Google AdMob, Google's Mobile Ads SDK may collect data for advertising purposes as described in the Advertising section below. See how Google uses data.

The only data stored is local to your device, including:

  • Your calculator input values, saved locally so they persist between sessions (stored in your browser or device local storage).
  • Your theme preference (light or dark mode).
  • Your list of saved favorite calculators.

This information never leaves your device.

Advertising

Our iOS and Android apps display advertisements served by Google AdMob. Google's Mobile Ads SDK may use your device's Advertising Identifier to serve and measure ads, and may share it with Google and third-party ad partners.

  • On iOS, you will be asked to allow tracking when you first open the app (App Tracking Transparency prompt). If you decline, you will still receive ads, but they may be less personalized.
  • You can reset or limit ad tracking at any time in your device settings (Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking on iOS).
  • In the European Economic Area, the UK, and Switzerland, we use Google's consent tools (User Messaging Platform) to obtain consent before showing personalized ads.
  • Google's own data policies apply to ad data. See Google's Privacy Policy.

Third-Party Links

Some pages may include links to external websites (for example, product or hardware retailers). We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those websites. We encourage you to review the privacy policies of any third-party site you visit.

Local Storage

We use local storage on your device to remember your preferences and inputs. You can clear this data at any time by clearing your browser or app data.

Children's Privacy

FieldMath is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated "last updated" date.
