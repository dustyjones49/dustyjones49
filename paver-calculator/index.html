Formula

area_sqin = L × W × 144 paver_footprint = (paverL + gap) × (paverW + gap) paver_count = ceil(area_sqin / paver_footprint × 1.1) base_cf = L × W × (baseDepth / 12) base_cy = base_cf / 27

Example Calculation

10×12 ft patio with 6×6 in pavers and 1/8 in gap:
Area = 10 × 12 × 144 = 17,280 sq in
Paver footprint = (6 + 0.125) × (6 + 0.125) = 37.52 sq in
Pavers = ceil(17,280 / 37.52 × 1.1) = 507 pavers with 10% waste.
Base = 10×12×(4/12) / 27 = 1.48 cubic yards.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many pavers for a 12×12 patio?

With standard 6×6 in pavers and 1/8 in gap, you need about 590 pavers for a 12×12 ft patio including 10% waste for cuts and breakage. For 12×12 in pavers it's about 148 pavers.

How deep should paver base be?

Use 4–6 inches of compacted crushed stone (3/4 in minus) for walkways and 6–8 inches for driveways with heavier loads. Add 1 inch of bedding sand on top before laying pavers. Compact base in 2–3 inch lifts.

How much sand do I need between pavers?

Use polymeric sand swept into joints — one 50lb bag covers about 50 sq ft of 1/8 in joints. For 100 sq ft patio order 2–3 bags. Wet it down lightly after sweeping to activate the polymer and lock pavers in place.
