Frequently Asked Questions

How many square yards in a 12x12 room?
144 sq ft = 16 sq yd of carpet. Carpet rolls are typically 12 ft wide, so you'd buy 12x12 = 144 sq ft. With 10% waste for seams and pattern matching, order 158 sq ft or about 18 sq yd.
Do I need carpet padding?
Yes, always. Good padding extends carpet life 30-50%, adds comfort, and reduces noise. 6-8lb density padding is standard. Budget $0.50-1.50/sq ft for padding. Our calculator factors in the main carpet only — add padding separately.
How is carpet sold — sq ft or sq yd?
Carpet is priced by the square foot but sold in 12 ft wide rolls by the linear foot. Our calculator gives you total sq ft and sq yd. Always confirm roll width with your supplier — some come in 15 ft widths too.
