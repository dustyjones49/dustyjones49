Frequently Asked Questions

How many square feet in a roll of wallpaper?
A standard double roll is 20.5 inches wide by 33 feet long — about 56 sq ft. But with pattern matching and waste, actual coverage is 40-50 sq ft per roll. Our calculator accounts for your pattern repeat.
What is pattern repeat?
Pattern repeat is the vertical distance before the pattern repeats. A 12 in repeat means 12 inches of wallpaper per strip is lost to matching. Larger repeats = more waste. Random match patterns have no waste.
Paste-the-wall vs paste-the-paper?
Paste-the-wall is faster — you roll adhesive directly on the wall. Paste-the-paper requires a pasting table and soaking time. Both work well. Non-woven wallpapers use paste-the-wall method.
