Frequently Asked Questions

About 0.5-0.75 gallons per 4x8 sheet for a Level 4 finish. That's roughly one 4.5 gallon bucket per 6-8 sheets. Level 5 skim coat needs about 1 gallon per sheet. Use all-purpose compound for embedding tape and lightweight for topping coats.

Level 3: tape + one coat, good for texture or tile application. Level 4: tape + two coats, standard for flat paint and light textures. Level 5: tape + two coats + skim coat, required for gloss paint and areas with critical lighting like large windows. Most residential homes are finished to Level 4.

Yes — unopened premixed mud lasts about 9-12 months from manufacture date. Once opened, use within 2-3 months. Moldy or sour-smelling mud must be thrown out immediately. Setting-type powder (hot mud) lasts indefinitely if kept dry in sealed containers.
