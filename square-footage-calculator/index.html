FAQ

Multiply length × width in feet for rectangular rooms. For odd shapes, break into rectangles and add. A 12×10 room = 120 sq ft. Always add 5-10% waste for cuts, especially with flooring or tile.

9 square feet = 1 square yard. To convert: divide sq ft by 9. 120 sq ft = 13.3 sq yd. This is important because carpet and some flooring materials are priced by the square yard.

Measure each closet or alcove separately (L×W) and add to the main room area. For bay windows, measure the deepest and widest points. Irregular spaces are best broken into rectangles. Round up each measurement to the nearest 6 inches.
