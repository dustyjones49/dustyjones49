Frequently Asked Questions

How many linear feet per tube of caulk?
A 10 oz tube covers about 30-50 linear ft with a 1/8 in bead, or 20-25 ft with 1/4 in bead. For a standard room with baseboards, budget 1 tube per door frame and 1 per window. Buy 2 extra — dried caulk is useless.
Silicone vs acrylic caulk?
100% silicone is waterproof and flexible — use for tubs, showers, sinks, and exterior. Acrylic (paintable) caulk is for baseboards, trim, crown molding and interior gaps. Don't use acrylic in wet areas — it'll fail.
How to apply caulk properly?
Cut tip at 45° angle to match bead size. Push, don't pull the gun. Smooth immediately with a wet finger or caulk tool. Tool within 2-3 minutes for acrylic, 30-60 seconds for silicone. Tape both sides for pro-looking lines.
