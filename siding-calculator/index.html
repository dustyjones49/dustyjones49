Frequently Asked Questions

A 2000 sq ft ranch with 8 ft ceilings has about 150 linear ft of perimeter for 1200 sq ft of wall area plus gables. That's 13-15 squares. Two-story houses have less wall per sq ft of living area. Our calculator handles all shapes — just measure your perimeter.

Vinyl siding costs $3-8 per sq ft installed vs $6-12 for fiber cement (Hardie). Vinyl is cheaper and zero-maintenance but can crack in extreme cold. Fiber cement lasts 50 years, is fireproof and insect-proof, and looks like real wood. Mid-range choice: engineered wood at $4-7/sq ft.

One square (100 sq ft) of standard double 4-inch vinyl siding is about 16-18 pieces at 12 ft long with 8-inch exposure. Fiber cement lap siding in 12 ft lengths needs about 14-16 pieces per square depending on exposure. Always check manufacturer specs for exact coverage per piece.
