Span = 12 ft. Use 2-ply 1-3/4x11-7/8 LVL minimum. Check local snow load requirements. Larger spans need 3-ply or deeper beams.

Only for very short spans under 8 feet. LVL or glulam is required for structural ridge beams in most modern construction. Always consult a structural engineer.
