Landscaping Cost Guide

Cost Overview

Landscaping costs $4-12 per sq ft for a full design and install. A basic front yard makeover runs $3,000-8,000. Full backyard landscaping with hardscaping, plantings, and lighting can cost $10,000-50,000. Professional design plans alone cost $500-3,000.

Factors That Affect Cost

  • Property size and slope
  • Plant maturity (small containers vs large specimens)
  • Hardscaping extent (retaining walls, walkways)
  • Irrigation system inclusion
  • Designer vs DIY landscape plan

Money-Saving Tips

  • Phase the project over 2-3 years to spread costs
  • Buy smaller plants (they grow fast in first 2 seasons)
  • Do your own planting to save 30-50% on labor
  • Use native plants (less water, fewer replacements)
  • Check with nursery for end-of-season clearance (Sept-Oct)
