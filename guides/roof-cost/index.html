2026 Pricing — Per Square by Material & Tear-Off Included

Overview

Roof replacement costs $450–900 per square (100 sq ft) for asphalt shingles. A typical 2,000 sq ft roof costs $9,000–18,000. This includes tear-off of old shingles, underlayment, flashing, and installation. Material choice is the single biggest cost driver.

Factors Affecting Cost

Material Type
3-tab is cheapest; architectural shingles offer better looks and wind resistance; metal and tile are lifetime investments.
Roof Pitch
Steeper roofs (8/12 and above) require safety harnesses, roof jacks, and more labor time — adding 20–40%.
Number of Layers to Tear Off
Most codes limit to 2 layers. Tearing off 2+ layers can double disposal fees and labor for that phase.
Accessibility & Height
Multi-story homes, steep lots, and poor truck access increase labor and equipment costs.
Chimney & Skylight Flashing
Each penetration needs custom flashing work, adding $200–500 per chimney or skylight.
Region
Coastal areas require wind-rated shingles; northern states need ice & water shield. Regional codes affect specs and cost.

Tips to Save Money

  • Get 4+ quotes — roofing is competitive; bids on the same job commonly vary 30–50%.
  • Avoid roofers after storms (storm chasers) — out-of-town crews inflate prices and may not honor warranties. Hire local, established companies.
  • Architectural shingles are the best value — only $100–200 more per square than 3-tab but last 10+ years longer and look significantly better.
  • Check insurance coverage if storm damage — hail and wind claims may cover a full replacement minus your deductible.
  • Replace in summer — shingles seal properly in warm weather, and crews can work faster with longer daylight hours.

Regional Price Variation

Construction costs vary significantly by region due to labor rates, material availability, and local demand. Here's how roofing pricing compares across U.S. regions:

  • Northeast: 10-20% above national average — higher labor, ice and water shield required by code
  • Southeast: 5-15% below national average — lower labor, but hurricane wind-rated shingles may add cost
  • Midwest: Near national average — competitive market, storm chasers keep prices in check
  • West Coast: 15-30% above national average — highest labor, fire-resistant materials in wildfire zones
  • Southwest: 5-10% below national average — year-round roofing keeps demand steady

Always get 3+ local quotes. National averages are a starting point — your specific zip code, site conditions, and current material prices determine the final cost.

When to Schedule Your Project

Best time: SUMMER or EARLY FALL — warm weather allows shingles to seal properly and crews work faster with longer daylight hours. Late summer/fall offers good balance of weather and availability.

Avoid: Winter (shingles become brittle and crack, ice hazards on roof), peak late spring rain seasons (weather delays can stretch the job). Storm season makes scheduling unpredictable.

Many roofers offer 5-10% discounts for fall projects to fill schedules before winter. Always hire local, established companies — avoid out-of-town storm chasers who inflate prices.

FAQ

How many squares is a 2,000 sq ft roof?

A 2,000 sq ft footprint house with a 6/12 pitch has about 2,236 sq ft of actual roof area, or about 22–23 squares. Our roof calculator can help with exact pitch-adjusted area — always measure actual roof surface, not the home footprint.

How long does a roof replacement take?

1–3 days for asphalt shingles on an average house. Metal roofs take 3–5 days. Tile roofs 5–10 days. Weather delays are common — always budget buffer days, especially during spring and fall rain seasons.
