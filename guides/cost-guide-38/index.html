Pole Barn Cost Guide 2025

Cost Overview

A pole barn — a post-frame structure built with wood posts set in the ground or on concrete piers — costs $20,000 to $60,000 for a basic building with a concrete floor. The cost per square foot runs $15 to $45 depending on size, with larger barns costing less per square foot due to economies of scale. Pole barns are popular for equipment storage, workshops, hay storage, livestock shelter, and increasingly as the shell for barndominiums.

Factors That Affect Cost

  • Building size: Cost per square foot decreases as the building gets larger. A 24x30 barn may cost $25/sq ft while a 40x60 barn costs $18/sq ft due to shared posts, trusses, and reduced trim linear footage.
  • Siding material: Corrugated steel is the most common and affordable at $3-$6 per sq ft. Board-and-batten wood siding costs $6-$12 per sq ft and requires more maintenance but looks more traditional.
  • Roof pitch and style: A standard 4:12 gable roof is economical; increasing the pitch for overhead storage or adding cupolas and ridge vents adds material and framing complexity.
  • Doors and openings: A large sliding barn door costs $1,000-$3,000; an overhead garage door costs $1,500-$4,000. Multiple openings increase framing and header costs.

Money-Saving Tips

  • Build in spring or fall when contractors have more availability — peak summer demand can increase labor costs by 10-20% for post-frame crews.
  • Use 4-foot on-center post spacing with 2x4 girts instead of 8-foot spacing with 2x6 girts; it uses less material overall and meets code in most wind zones.
  • Pour the concrete slab after the building is erected so the posts are already in place and the slab can serve as the floor without needing separate footings for posts.
  • Choose a clear-span truss design that eliminates interior posts; the truss cost is slightly higher but you gain 100% usable floor space with no obstructions.
  • Opt for galvalume steel siding instead of painted steel for an agricultural building; the raw metallic finish costs 15-20% less and develops a uniform patina over time.
