Paver Patio Cost Guide

Cost Overview

Installing a paver patio costs $15-40 per square foot including materials and labor. A 20×20 ft (400 sq ft) patio averages $6,000-16,000. Basic concrete pavers are most affordable while natural stone like travertine and flagstone sits at the high end.

Factors That Affect Cost

  • Paver material and thickness
  • Base depth (4-12 in of gravel/sand)
  • Excavation and site prep
  • Retaining walls needed for slopes
  • Complex patterns vs straight layout

Money-Saving Tips

  • Choose concrete over natural stone (40% less)
  • Keep a simple rectangular layout
  • DIY the excavation to save $500-1,500
  • Buy pavers in bulk or overstock lots
  • Schedule in fall when contractors are less busy
