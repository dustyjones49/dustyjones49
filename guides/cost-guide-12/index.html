Garage Build Cost Guide

Cost Overview

Building a garage costs $50-100 per square foot. A standard 2-car detached garage (24×24, 576 sq ft) runs $28,000-57,000. Attached garages cost 10-20% less because one wall is shared. A 3-car garage adds roughly 50% more square footage and cost.

Factors That Affect Cost

  • Attached vs detached construction
  • Foundation/slab requirements
  • Electrical and lighting package
  • Insulation and drywall finish
  • Garage door quality and quantity

Money-Saving Tips

  • Build attached to share a wall with existing structure
  • Skip drywall and insulation for storage-only garages
  • Use standard-sized garage doors (9×7 or 16×7)
  • Pour slab in conjunction with driveway for bulk concrete pricing
  • Pre-fab garage kits can save 20-30% over custom builds
