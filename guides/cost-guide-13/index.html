Driveway Paving Cost Guide

Cost Overview

A new driveway costs $2,500-10,000 on average. Gravel is cheapest at $1-3 per sq ft. Asphalt is a mid-range option at $3-6 per sq ft. Concrete driveways cost $5-10 per sq ft while paver driveways are the premium choice at $10-25 per sq ft.

Factors That Affect Cost

  • Excavation and grading requirements
  • Base material depth and type
  • Climate (freeze-thaw cycles affect longevity)
  • Driveway length and slope
  • Removal of old driveway

Money-Saving Tips

  • Gravel is budget-friendly and lasts 100+ years with maintenance
  • Asphalt is most cost-effective for long driveways
  • Seal asphalt every 2-3 years (extends life by 50%)
  • Combine with neighbor’s project for contractor discounts
  • DIY gravel driveway installation to eliminate labor costs
