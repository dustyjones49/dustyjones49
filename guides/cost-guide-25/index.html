Outdoor Living Space Cost Guide

Cost Overview

An outdoor living space costs $5,000-50,000+ depending on features. A basic paver patio with a fire pit runs $5,000-10,000. A full outdoor kitchen with grill, refrigerator, sink and pergola can reach $15,000-40,000. High-end builds with covered structures exceed $50,000.

Factors That Affect Cost

  • Covered vs uncovered structures
  • Appliance quality (built-in vs freestanding grill)
  • Utility runs (gas, electrical, water)
  • Permits (electrical/plumbing)
  • Seating walls and built-in features

Money-Saving Tips

  • Build in phases: patio first, kitchen next year
  • Freestanding grill saves $3-8k vs built-in
  • Gravel patio instead of pavers (costs $1-3/sq ft)
  • DIY fire pit (prefab kit is $200-500)
  • String lights for ambiance instead of hardwired lighting
