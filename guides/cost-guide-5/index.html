Fence Installation Cost Guide

Cost Overview

Wood privacy fence costs $15-30 per linear foot installed. A 150 ft backyard fence runs $2,250-4,500. Vinyl costs $20-40/ft, chain link $10-20/ft, and composite $25-45/ft. Gate installations add $200-500 each.

Factors That Affect Cost

  • Fence height
  • Material choice
  • Terrain and slope
  • Number of gates
  • Post setting method
  • Old fence removal
  • Property line survey

Money-Saving Tips

  • Get 3+ quotes
  • Call 811 before digging
  • Buy materials in winter (10-20% off)
  • DIY post holes (saves $5-10/ft)
  • Share fence costs with neighbors
