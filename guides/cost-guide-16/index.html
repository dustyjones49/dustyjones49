Countertop Cost Guide

Cost Overview

Countertop installation costs $30-100 per sq ft depending on the material. Laminate starts at $15-35 per sq ft installed. Granite and quartz are the sweet spot at $45-80 per sq ft for a standard kitchen. Marble and quartzite push into luxury territory at $70-150 per sq ft.

Factors That Affect Cost

  • Slab size and seam count
  • Edge profile (eased vs waterfall)
  • Sink cutout and faucet drill holes
  • Backsplash matching
  • Transport weight for natural stone

Money-Saving Tips

  • Quartz is more consistent and waste-free vs natural stone
  • Choose a tier 1 color (most affordable in any stone line)
  • Look for remnant pieces for small bathrooms or islands
  • Standard eased edge is included; fancy edges are upgrades
  • Buy during January/February when fabricators have less work
