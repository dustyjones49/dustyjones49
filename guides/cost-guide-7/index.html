Roof Replacement Cost Guide

Cost Overview

A new roof costs $5,500-12,000 for a standard 2,000 sq ft home with asphalt shingles. Metal roofing runs $10,000-30,000, and slate or tile can exceed $25,000-50,000. Most shingle roofs last 20-30 years with proper installation.

Factors That Affect Cost

  • Roof pitch and complexity (valleys, dormers)
  • Number of stories
  • Old roof tear-off and disposal
  • Sheathing replacement if rotted
  • Local climate and code requirements

Money-Saving Tips

  • Replace in winter or early spring (off-season discounts)
  • Avoid overlays—tear off old shingles for proper inspection
  • Get insurance to cover storm-damaged roofs
  • Compare multiple roofing contractors with detailed bids
  • Consider architectural shingles for best value-to-longevity ratio
