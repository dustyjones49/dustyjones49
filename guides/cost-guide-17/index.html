Cabinet Cost Guide

Cost Overview

Kitchen cabinets cost $150-500 per linear foot depending on quality tier. A standard 10×10 kitchen with 25 linear feet of cabinetry runs $3,750-12,500 for stock cabinets. Semi-custom doubles the cost and fully custom can triple or quadruple it. Cabinets are typically 30-40% of a kitchen remodel budget.

Factors That Affect Cost

  • Wood species (oak/maple vs cherry/walnut)
  • Door style (slab, shaker, raised panel)
  • Finish (painted, stained, glazed)
  • Soft-close hardware
  • Upper to lower cabinet ratio

Money-Saving Tips

  • RTA (ready-to-assemble) cabinets cost 30-50% less than assembled
  • Skip glass-front doors and specialty inserts
  • Open shelving can replace some upper cabinets
  • IKEA is popular for budget builds with modern style
  • Buy during February new model clearances
