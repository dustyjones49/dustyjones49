Flooring Installation Cost Guide

Cost Overview

Flooring installation costs $3-15 per sq ft including labor and materials. Carpet is the most affordable at $3-7 per sq ft. LVP (luxury vinyl plank) costs $4-9 per sq ft. Hardwood floors range from $8-15 per sq ft installed and can last 50+ years with refinishing.

Factors That Affect Cost

  • Removal of old flooring ($1-3/sq ft)
  • Subfloor condition and repairs
  • Stairs and transitions add complexity
  • Moisture barrier for concrete slabs
  • Furniture moving fees

Money-Saving Tips

  • LVP is DIY-friendly with click-lock planks
  • Buy flooring during holiday sales (10-20% off)
  • Remove old carpet yourself to save on demo
  • Engineered vs solid hardwood saves $2-4/sq ft
  • Move furniture yourself before installers arrive
