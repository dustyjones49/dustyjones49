Siding Replacement Cost Guide

Cost Overview

Siding replacement costs $6-12 per sq ft installed. A 1,500 sq ft exterior wall area runs $9,000-18,000. Vinyl is the most affordable option, while fiber cement (Hardie board) and engineered wood sit in the mid-range. Cedar and real wood are premium choices.

Factors That Affect Cost

  • Total exterior wall square footage
  • Old siding removal and disposal
  • House wrap and insulation upgrades
  • Trim, soffit and fascia replacement
  • Multi-story homes (scaffolding/equipment)

Money-Saving Tips

  • Vinyl offers best upfront cost-to-durability ratio
  • Install in off-season late fall or early spring
  • Keep existing trim if in good condition
  • Bundle with window replacement for contractor discounts
  • Insulated vinyl can reduce energy bills by 10-15%
