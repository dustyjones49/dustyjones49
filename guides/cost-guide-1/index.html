Kitchen Remodel Cost Guide

Cost Overview

A kitchen remodel costs $25,000-60,000 on average for a mid-range renovation. Budget refreshes start at $10,000, while luxury renovations with custom cabinetry can exceed $100,000. The average return on investment is 60-80%.

Factors That Affect Cost

  • Kitchen size (sq ft)
  • Cabinet quality (stock vs custom)
  • Countertop material (laminate vs quartz)
  • Appliance brands
  • Moving plumbing/gas lines
  • Structural changes (removing walls)
  • Permits and inspections
  • Regional labor rates

Money-Saving Tips

  • Refinish existing cabinets instead of replacing (saves $5-15k)
  • Keep the same footprint to avoid moving plumbing
  • Shop floor model and scratch-and-dent appliances
  • Do demo yourself (saves $1-2k)
  • Get 3+ itemized bids
  • Buy cabinets during holiday sales
