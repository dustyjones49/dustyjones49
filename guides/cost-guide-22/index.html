Sod Installation Cost Guide

Cost Overview

Sod installation costs $1-2 per sq ft on average. Sod pallets cover 450-500 sq ft and cost $300-500 each. A 1,000 sq ft lawn costs $1,000-2,000 installed. Sod provides instant curb appeal vs seeding which takes 4-8 weeks and costs 80% less.

Factors That Affect Cost

  • Grass type (Bermuda, St. Augustine, Fescue, Zoysia)
  • Delivery distance from sod farm
  • Site prep (old grass removal, grading)
  • Accessibility (pallet drops, carrying sod rolls)
  • Season (peak spring/summer demand)

Money-Saving Tips

  • Pick up sod yourself to avoid delivery fees
  • Install within 24 hours of cutting for best survival
  • Seed instead of sod for large lawns (costs $0.05-0.10/sq ft)
  • Water heavily for first 2 weeks to prevent die-off
  • Rent a sod cutter for DIY removal of old grass
