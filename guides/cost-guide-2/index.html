Bathroom Remodel Cost Guide

Cost Overview

A bathroom remodel costs $6,000-15,000 for a full gut renovation of a standard 5×8 ft bathroom. Half baths cost $3,000-7,000. Master bathrooms with luxury finishes run $15,000-40,000. ROI is 60-70% at resale.

Factors That Affect Cost

  • Bathroom size
  • Moving plumbing (adds $3-5k)
  • Tile choice vs fiberglass surround
  • Heated floors (+$2-4k)
  • Custom vs stock vanity
  • Ventilation requirements
  • Waterproofing standards
  • Labor vs DIY

Money-Saving Tips

  • Use stock vanity instead of custom (saves $1-3k)
  • Keep toilet and shower in same location
  • Fiberglass surround costs 1/3 of tile
  • DIY demo saves $500-1,000
  • Buy fixtures as a set for 10-15% discount
