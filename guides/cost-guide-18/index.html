Paint Job Cost Guide

Cost Overview

Interior painting costs $2-6 per sq ft for walls and $1-3 per sq ft for ceilings. A 12×12 room averages $400-900 including labor and materials. Exterior painting runs $2,500-7,000 for a 2,000 sq ft home. Trim and doors add $50-100 each.

Factors That Affect Cost

  • Ceiling height (vaulted ceilings add 20-40%)
  • Paint quality (contractor vs premium grade)
  • Wall condition (repairs, texture matching)
  • Color changes (dark to light needs more coats)
  • Accessibility (stairwells, high walls)

Money-Saving Tips

  • DIY is the biggest saver—labor is 70-85% of cost
  • Buy paint during Memorial Day/Labor Day sales (30-40% off)
  • Primer + paint combos save a separate step
  • Paint trim and doors the same color (reduces cutting time)
  • Do your own prep: fill holes, sand, tape, and cover floors
