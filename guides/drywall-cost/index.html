Drywall Cost Guide

2026 Pricing — Per Sheet, Per Sq Ft & Finish Levels

Overview

Drywall installation costs $1.50–3 per sq ft finished (hung, taped, mudded). A 12×14 room with 8 ft ceilings (584 sq ft of wall and ceiling area) costs $876–1,752. Labor is the majority of cost; hanging is the quick part, finishing is where the skill matters.

Factors Affecting Cost

Room Size & Complexity
Small rooms with many corners and angles cost more per sq ft than large open spaces due to setup and cutting time.
Ceiling Height
Walls over 8 ft require 54" wide sheets or horizontal stacking, increasing labor. Cathedral ceilings cost significantly more.
Finish Level (1–5)
Level 4 is standard for flat paint; Level 5 (skim coat entire surface) is required for gloss paint and high-light areas.
Corners & Windows
Each inside corner, outside corner, and window return adds taping and mudding labor. Open floor plans are cheaper to drywall.
Region
Labor rates vary from $0.30–0.80/sq ft for hanging depending on market. Union markets cost 20–30% more.
Old Drywall Removal
Demolition and disposal of existing drywall adds $0.50–1.00/sq ft. Factor this in for renovation projects.

Tips to Save Money

  • DIY hanging saves 30–40% — but taping is harder than it looks. Many DIYers hang themselves and hire a pro finisher.
  • Use 4×12 sheets for fewer seams — on 9 ft walls, a single 54"×12 ft sheet covers an entire wall with zero horizontal seams.
  • Level 4 finish is standard for flat paint — don't pay for Level 5 unless you're using high-gloss or enamel paint in bright rooms.
  • Rent a drywall lift ($35/day) — holding 4×12 sheets overhead is nearly impossible solo. A lift is the best $35 you'll spend.

Regional Price Variation

Construction costs vary significantly by region due to labor rates, material availability, and local demand. Here's how drywall pricing compares across U.S. regions:

  • Northeast: 10-20% above national average — higher labor and union rates dominate
  • Southeast: 5-15% below national average — lower labor costs, competitive market
  • Midwest: Near national average — competitive rates, steady construction demand
  • West Coast: 15-30% above national average — highest labor rates, seismic codes add complexity
  • Southwest: 5-10% below national average — competitive market, steady year-round construction

Always get 3+ local quotes. National averages are a starting point — your specific zip code, site conditions, and current material prices determine the final cost.

When to Schedule Your Project

Best time: ANY SEASON — drywall is indoor work. Control interior temperature and humidity for proper mud drying. Winter is actually ideal: contractors have lower demand and may offer 10-15% discounts.

Avoid: Scheduling during other major renovations (dust from framing, sanding, or painting creates conflicts). High humidity slows mud drying and can cause joint compound issues.

Many drywall crews offer off-peak discounts during winter months when new construction slows down. Ask about winter rates.

FAQ

How much does it cost to drywall a 12×12 room?

A 12×12×8 room has 384 sq ft of walls and 144 sq ft of ceiling = 528 sq ft total. At $1.50–3/sq ft complete, that's $792–1,584. That's about 17 sheets of standard 4×8 drywall.

Can I drywall myself to save money?

Hanging is DIY-friendly and saves $0.50–1/sq ft. Taping/mudding takes practice to get smooth — many DIYers hang themselves and hire a finisher for $0.50–0.80/sq ft for the taping only. It's the best cost-quality compromise.
