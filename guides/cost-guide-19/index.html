Drywall Installation Cost Guide

Cost Overview

Drywall installation costs $1.50-3.50 per sq ft including hanging, taping, mudding and finishing. A 12×12 room (400 sq ft of drywall) costs $600-1,400. Ceilings cost more than walls. Textured finishes add $0.50-1 per sq ft on top of standard smooth finish.

Factors That Affect Cost

  • Finish level (3 through 5; higher = smoother)
  • Ceiling height and lift rental
  • Number of corners, arches and bulkheads
  • Old drywall removal and disposal
  • Moisture-resistant or fire-rated board

Money-Saving Tips

  • Level 4 finish is standard and adequate for most homes
  • Hang it yourself, hire a pro for taping/mudding
  • Buy 4×12 sheets to reduce butt joints (less mudding)
  • Use 1/2-inch regular for walls (no benefit to 5/8 in most rooms)
  • Remove old drywall yourself to save disposal fees
