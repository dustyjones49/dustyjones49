Insulation Cost Guide

Cost Overview

Home insulation costs $1,500-4,500 for an average attic of 1,500 sq ft. Blown-in fiberglass or cellulose is $0.60-1.50 per sq ft. Spray foam is most expensive at $1.50-4 per sq ft but offers the highest R-value per inch. Proper insulation can reduce energy bills by 15-25%.

Factors That Affect Cost

  • Square footage and target R-value
  • Access difficulty (crawl space, attic with low clearance)
  • Air sealing before insulating
  • Old insulation removal
  • Climate zone requirements

Money-Saving Tips

  • DIY fiberglass batts in accessible attics (save 50-60%)
  • Many utility companies offer rebates for insulation upgrades
  • Cellulose is often cheapest for retrofit
  • Air seal first; insulation is far less effective without it
  • $1,200 Federal tax credit for qualifying insulation
