Pool Installation Cost Guide

Cost Overview

An inground pool costs $35,000-65,000 on average. Fiberglass pools are the most affordable inground option at $35,000-55,000. Concrete (gunite) pools run $50,000-100,000 and take 2-3 months to install. Above-ground pools cost $3,000-10,000 and can be a DIY weekend project.

Factors That Affect Cost

  • Pool size and depth
  • Excavation difficulty (soil, rock, access)
  • Decking and coping materials
  • Fencing requirements (safety code)
  • Additional features (hot tub, waterfall, heater)

Money-Saving Tips

  • Choose fiberglass over gunite (saves $15-30k)
  • Above-ground pools are 80-90% cheaper and relocatable
  • Install in fall/winter for off-season contractor discounts
  • Keep a simple shape (rectangle); freeform adds 15-30%
  • Budget $1,200-3,000/year for ongoing maintenance
