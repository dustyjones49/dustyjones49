Water Heater Cost Guide

Cost Overview

Water heater replacement costs $900-2,500 including installation. Tank water heaters are least expensive upfront. Tankless models cost $1,500-4,500 installed but are 24-34% more energy efficient and last 20+ years. Electric heat pump water heaters are the most efficient option.

Factors That Affect Cost

  • Fuel type (gas requires venting and gas line)
  • Tank capacity (gallons)
  • Location (basement vs attic access)
  • Permit requirements
  • Gallon per minute (GPM) for tankless

Money-Saving Tips

  • Heat pump water heaters qualify for $2,000 Federal tax credit
  • Flush tank annually to extend lifespan 3-5 years
  • Insulate hot water pipes (saves 3-4% on water heating bill)
  • Replace before failure to avoid emergency pricing
  • Right-size the tank; oversized wastes energy
