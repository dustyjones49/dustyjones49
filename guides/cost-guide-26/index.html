Mudroom Cost Guide 2025

Cost Overview

A mudroom keeps dirt, wet shoes, and outdoor gear contained before they enter your main living space. A basic DIY setup with a bench and wall hooks can cost as little as $1,000, while a full-scale custom mudroom with built-in lockers, cubbies, tile flooring, and bench seating runs $5,000 to $15,000. Most homeowners spend between $2,500 and $8,000 for a mid-range mudroom with prefabricated cabinetry and durable flooring.

Factors That Affect Cost

  • Cabinetry type: Stock or prefab units cost far less than fully custom millwork with dovetail drawers and soft-close hardware.
  • Wall and floor finishes: Wainscoting, beadboard, ceramic tile, or luxury vinyl plank all carry different material and labor costs.
  • Number of stations: A 2-person mudroom with two lockers costs less than a family setup with 4-6 personalized cubbies and charging stations.
  • Plumbing additions: Adding a utility sink or dog-wash station requires rough-in plumbing that can add $1,500 to $4,000.
  • Electrical work: Overhead lighting, task lights inside each cubby, and USB outlets increase both material and electrician labor costs.

Money-Saving Tips

  • Use stock kitchen pantry cabinets as mudroom lockers — they are 30-50% cheaper than custom built-ins and come in matching finishes.
  • Install durable luxury vinyl plank flooring yourself rather than paying for professional tile installation; it handles moisture well and costs about $3/sq ft.
  • Paint existing drywall instead of adding beadboard or shiplap to save hundreds on materials and labor.
  • Shop for bench seating at furniture outlets or build a simple plywood bench topped with a foam cushion — far cheaper than a custom upholstered bench.
  • Limit the number of electric outlets to one per two lockers to trim electrician hours without sacrificing practicality.
