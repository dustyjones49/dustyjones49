Home Office Cost Guide 2025

Cost Overview

Converting a spare bedroom or bonus space into a dedicated home office costs $2,000 to $10,000 depending on the level of finish. A simple setup with a store-bought desk, a fresh coat of paint, and a new light fixture falls on the low end, while a full build-out includes custom built-in cabinetry, recessed lighting, soundproofing, and dedicated circuits for computer equipment. Most professionals spending 40+ hours a week in a home office invest $5,000 to $8,000 for ergonomic, well-lit workspaces.

Factors That Affect Cost

  • Desk configuration: A single straight run of desk is the cheapest; L-shaped, U-shaped, or floating desks with multiple workstations add material and labor costs quickly.
  • Built-in vs. freestanding furniture: Custom built-ins cost 3-5x more than quality freestanding desks but maximize floor space and increase resale value.
  • Electrical needs: A home office with multiple monitors, printers, and networking gear often needs 2-3 dedicated 20-amp circuits, which costs $300-$700 per circuit.
  • Natural light and window treatments: Replacing a small window or adding solar shades for glare control adds $400-$1,200.
  • Flooring: Carpet is the most affordable office flooring at $2-$5/sq ft; hardwood or engineered wood runs $6-$15/sq ft installed.

Money-Saving Tips

  • Use IKEA or similar modular cabinet systems configured as built-ins — they cost about $1,000-$2,000 for a full wall setup versus $5,000+ for custom millwork.
  • Run Ethernet cables yourself if you have an unfinished basement or attic for access; CAT6 cable is under $0.20/ft and you save $75-$150 per drop on labor.
  • Install dimmable LED wafer lights instead of traditional recessed cans — they require no housing, are DIY-friendly, and cost as little as $15 each.
  • Add acoustic panels only on the wall behind your desk and the opposite wall rather than covering every surface; strategic placement provides 80% of the benefit at 30% of the cost.
  • Paint the room yourself with a high-quality matte finish in a calming color — a $60 gallon of paint transforms a room as effectively as $3,000 worth of millwork.
