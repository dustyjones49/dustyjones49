Formula

area = L × H
blocks_clean = ceil(area / 0.89)
blocks_with_waste = ceil(blocks_clean × (1 + waste% / 100))
mortar_bags = ceil(blocks_with_waste / 33)

Example Calculation

A 40 × 8 ft wall with 5% waste:
Area = 40 × 8 = 320 sq ft
Blocks (clean) = ceil(320 / 0.89) = 360 blocks
Blocks (5% waste) = ceil(360 × 1.05) = 378 blocks
Mortar = ceil(378 / 33) ≈ 12 bags

FAQ

Standard 8x16 blocks cover 0.89 sq ft each with mortar joint, so you need 113 blocks per 100 sq ft of wall. With 5% waste, order 119 blocks per 100 sq ft. Half blocks fill at corners and ends.
You need about 3 bags of 80lb mortar per 100 blocks for typical 3/8 inch joints. Our calculator estimates block count only. Add 1 bag of mortar per 35-40 blocks when ordering materials.
Residential block walls under 4 ft tall generally don't need rebar if on solid footing. Walls over 4 ft or retaining walls need vertical rebar at 32 in oc in every other core and horizontal bond beam every 2-3 courses.
