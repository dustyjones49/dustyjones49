FAQ

Use 60% topsoil + 30% compost + 10% perlite or vermiculite. Don't use 100% bagged potting mix—it's too light and expensive for large beds. For veggies, add 2 inches of compost annually.
Minimum 6 inches for lettuce and shallow roots. 12 inches for tomatoes, peppers, and most vegetables. 18-24 inches for root crops like carrots and potatoes. Deeper is always better for root development and moisture retention.
Topsoil is cheap fill with some organic matter. Garden soil is enriched topsoil with compost added. Potting soil is lightweight soilless mix for containers. For raised beds: buy bulk garden soil blend by the yard—it's 40-60% cheaper than bagged.
