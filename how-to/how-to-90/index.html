How to Clean Stainless Steel Appliances

Overview

Stainless steel resists stains but shows fingerprints, smudges, and water spots easily. Cleaning with the grain and using the right products keeps appliances looking new without scratching or dulling the brushed finish.

Tools & Materials

  • Microfiber cloths (at least 2—one for cleaning, one for buffing)
  • Mild dish soap (non-abrasive, clear)
  • White vinegar (for disinfecting and de-greasing)
  • Baby oil, mineral oil, or commercial stainless steel polish
  • Club soda (for streak-free cleaning)
  • Spray bottle
  • Soft sponge

Step-by-Step

  1. Identify the grain direction. Look closely at the surface in bright light. You'll see faint parallel lines running in one direction. Always wipe parallel to the grain, never against it. Wiping perpendicular pushes dirt into the grain's tiny grooves.
  2. Start with soap and water. Mix a few drops of mild dish soap with warm water. Dampen a microfiber cloth with the solution and wring it out so it's barely damp. Wipe the appliance surface following the grain.
  3. Rinse with clean water. Use a second microfiber cloth dampened with plain water to wipe off any soap residue. Soap film left on stainless steel looks dull and attracts fingerprints.
  4. Dry immediately. Buff the surface dry with a clean, dry microfiber cloth, following the grain. Water spots form within seconds on stainless steel if it air-dries.
  5. For grease and fingerprints. Mix equal parts white vinegar and water in a spray bottle. Spray lightly onto the cloth (not directly on the appliance) and wipe with the grain. Vinegar cuts grease and kills bacteria.
  6. Club soda trick. For a streak-free shine, put club soda in a spray bottle and spray onto a microfiber cloth. Wipe with the grain and buff dry. The carbonation lifts dirt without leaving mineral deposits.
  7. Apply a protective coat. Once the surface is clean and dry, apply a tiny amount of baby oil or mineral oil to a clean cloth and buff over the surface following the grain. This creates a thin protective layer that repels fingerprints. Wipe off any excess with a dry cloth.
  8. Use commercial polish (optional). Spray stainless steel polish onto the cloth, apply with the grain, and buff to a shine. Products like Weiman or 3M leave a protective coating lasting several weeks.

Pro Tips

Tip 1: Never use steel wool, abrasive scrub pads, bleach, or ammonia-based cleaners on stainless steel. They permanently scratch the surface and dull the finish.
Tip 2: Never use glass cleaner with ammonia on stainless steel. Ammonia can discolor and damage the protective clear coat on some stainless appliances.
Tip 3: For badly neglected appliances with baked-on grease, make a paste of baking soda and water. Apply gently with a soft sponge following the grain, then rinse and dry thoroughly. Baking soda is mild enough to use occasionally.
Tip 4: The bottom of refrigerator and dishwasher stainless panels are "fingerprint resistant" on most modern appliances. Clean these areas gently—aggressive scrubbing can wear off the factory coating.
