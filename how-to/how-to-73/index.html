How to Replace a Refrigerator Water Filter

Overview

Replacing the refrigerator water filter every 6 months ensures clean-tasting water and ice and protects the water system from sediment and contaminants. Most filters are either push-in or twist-in types and take less than 5 minutes to swap.

Tools & Materials

  • Replacement water filter (model-specific—check fridge model number)
  • Towel or paper towels (for drips)
  • Small bowl or cup (for dripping water)

Step-by-Step

  1. Buy the correct replacement. Find your refrigerator's model number (usually on a sticker inside the fresh food compartment) and cross-reference it with the correct filter part number. Generic aftermarket filters work fine and cost less than OEM.
  2. Locate the old filter. The filter is typically in one of three places: inside the refrigerator at the top right or left corner, behind the base grille at the bottom front, or in the lower left kickplate area.
  3. For push-button filters: Press the release button next to the filter. The filter will pop out. Some models require you to push in first, then release.
  4. For twist-in filters: Turn the filter counterclockwise one-quarter turn. It will unlock and drop down. Pull it straight out.
  5. For grille-mounted filters: Remove the base grille by pulling it from the bottom. Turn the filter counterclockwise and pull out.
  6. Expect some water. Hold a towel under the filter as you remove it. A small amount of water (1/4 cup) will drain from the filter head. It's normal.
  7. Remove the filter cap. Some filters have a cap or cover that needs to be removed from the old filter and snapped onto the new one.
  8. Install the new filter. Insert the new filter and either push it in until it clicks or twist it clockwise until it locks. The arrow or mark on the filter should align with the locked position indicator.
  9. Flush the system. After installing, dispense 3–4 gallons of water through the water dispenser (about 5 minutes of running) to flush out air and carbon fines. The water may sputter and appear cloudy at first—that's normal.
  10. Reset the filter indicator. Press and hold the "Filter Reset" or "Water Filter" button on the control panel for 3 seconds until the indicator light turns green or the "Replace Filter" message disappears.

Pro Tips

Tip 1: Write the installation date on the new filter with a permanent marker before installing. When the 6-month reminder pops up, you'll know exactly how old it is.
Tip 2: Turn off the ice maker before replacing the filter to prevent air from being pulled into the ice maker line, which can cause small or misshapen cubes.
Tip 3: If the filter is stuck and hard to twist, turn off the water supply and dispense water for 30 seconds to relieve line pressure before trying again.
Tip 4: Subscribe to filter delivery every 6 months. It's cheaper than buying individually and you'll never forget to replace it.
