How to Clean a Washing Machine

Overview

Washing machines accumulate detergent residue, fabric softener buildup, mold, and mildew over time—especially high-efficiency (HE) front-loaders. A monthly cleaning cycle keeps your washer fresh and your clothes smelling clean.

Tools & Materials

  • Washing machine cleaner tablets or 2 cups white vinegar + 1/2 cup baking soda
  • Microfiber cloths
  • Soft-bristle brush or old toothbrush
  • Spray bottle with white vinegar or bleach solution (1:10 bleach to water)
  • Q-tips or cotton swabs
  • Bucket (for front-loader drain filter)

Step-by-Step

  1. Remove detergent dispensers. Slide out the detergent, bleach, and fabric softener trays. Soak them in hot, soapy water. Scrub with a brush to remove dried detergent and mold. Rinse and dry.
  2. Clean the dispenser housing. Use a damp cloth and Q-tips to clean inside the dispenser cavity. Built-up detergent paste is a breeding ground for mold.
  3. Clean the door gasket (front-loader). Pull back the rubber gasket seal around the door opening. Wipe the entire inner surface and folds with a cloth soaked in vinegar or bleach solution. Use Q-tips for the small crevices where mold hides.
  4. Clean the drain filter (front-loader). Locate the small access door at the bottom front of the machine. Open it, place a shallow bucket and towel underneath, and slowly unscrew the filter cap. Water will drain out. Remove the filter, rinse it, and reinstall.
  5. Run the cleaning cycle. Add a washing machine cleaner tablet according to package instructions, OR pour 2 cups of white vinegar into the drum and 1/2 cup of baking soda into the detergent dispenser (for top-loaders, add both directly to the tub).
  6. Select the clean cycle. Choose the "Clean Washer," "Sanitize," or the hottest and longest cycle available. Run it empty with no clothes.
  7. Wipe down after the cycle. When the cycle finishes, wipe the entire drum, gasket, and door glass with a dry microfiber cloth. Leave the door and dispenser drawer open to air-dry completely.
  8. Clean the exterior. Wipe down the control panel, lid, and exterior surfaces with a damp cloth. Avoid spraying cleaners directly on the control panel.

Pro Tips

Tip 1: Always leave the washer door and detergent drawer slightly open between loads. Front-loaders seal so tightly that moisture gets trapped and mold grows within 24–48 hours.
Tip 2: Use only HE (high-efficiency) detergent in HE machines and don't over-pour. Excess suds in the gasket and seals feed mold growth.
Tip 3: If the gasket has stubborn black mold stains, lay paper towels soaked in bleach across the stained areas and let them sit for 2 hours before wiping clean.
Tip 4: Run a monthly cleaning cycle. If you use homemade laundry soap, clean monthly as these soaps don't have the anti-scaling chemicals found in commercial detergents.
