FAQ

Most manufactured stone veneer boxes contain 8-10 sq ft of flats and cover about 7-8 sq ft after overlap. Corner pieces cover about 0.75 sq ft per linear foot. Always check the manufacturer's coverage chart.
Manufactured stone veneer costs $8-15/sq ft installed vs $25-40 for real stone. It's 75% lighter, easier to install, and available in more colors. Real stone adds the most resale value but requires a structural ledge.
Over plywood or OSB: yes—apply two layers of weather-resistant barrier, metal lath, and a 1/2-3/4 in scratch coat of Type S mortar. Over concrete block: just dampen and apply. Over existing brick: clean first, then apply directly.
