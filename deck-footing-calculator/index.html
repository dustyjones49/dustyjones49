FAQ

Below the frost line—check your local code. Most areas: 30-42 inches. Southern states: 12-18 inches. Add 6 in of gravel at the bottom of each hole for drainage. Footing must bear on undisturbed soil, not fill.
Minimum 12 in diameter for a 4x4 post, 14 in for a 6x6. The footing should be at least 2× the post width. Larger footings spread the load over more soil—increase size for heavy decks (hot tubs, stone tops) or poor soil.
Not required by most codes for residential decks under 6 ft tall. For taller decks or heavy loads, add 2 #4 vertical rebar bars in each footing tied to the post anchor. Rebar prevents the footing from cracking and separating.
