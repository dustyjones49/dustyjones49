FAQ

Standard: 18 inches from counter to upper cabinet bottoms. This includes a 4-inch standard splash plus 14 inches of decorative tile. Full backsplash goes counter to ceiling and needs about 3× more tile. Measure each wall section separately for L-shaped kitchens.

Popular sizes: 3×6 subway ($3-8/sq ft), 4×4 classic ($2-5), mosaic sheets ($5-15). Large format (12×24) looks modern and has fewer grout lines. Small mosaics cost more in labor but need fewer cuts around outlets.
