FAQ

Standard 8" CMU (concrete masonry units) sized 8x8x16" are most common. They provide a sturdy, fireproof structure for mounting grills and stone veneer.

Materials typically run $2,000-$5,000 for a basic island. Stone veneer, countertops, and appliances add significantly. Professional builds range from $5,000-$15,000.

Granite and concrete are most popular for outdoor use. Both handle weather well. Tile is a budget option. Always ensure proper overhang and sealing.
