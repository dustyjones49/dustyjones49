Irrigation Layout

Formula: heads = ceil(area / headCoverage) headsPerZone = floor(availGPM / headGPM) zones = ceil(heads / headsPerZone) Example: 5000 sq ft lawn, 100 sq ft per head, 10 GPM available, 2.5 GPM per head: 50 heads, 4 per zone, 13 zones, 3/4 in pipe.

FAQ

Divide available GPM by head GPM. A typical residential zone: 10 GPM / 2.5 GPM per head = 4 heads per zone. Stick to same type and brand in each zone for even coverage. 1 zone = 1 valve.
3/4 inch PVC for zones under 10 GPM. 1 inch for 10-15 GPM. 1-1/4 inch for 15-20 GPM. Main line carries all zones combined and is usually 1 inch or larger. Use Schedule 40 PVC rated for pressure.
DIY: $1,500-3,000 for an average 5,000 sq ft lawn (heads, pipe, controller, backflow preventer). Professional install: $3,000-6,000 or $0.60-1.20/sq ft. The controller is $50-200 and a backflow preventer is required by code ($100-300).
